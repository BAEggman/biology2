The attached picture is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not redraw it and do not copy its contents. Draw a COMPLETELY NEW picture in exactly the same style as the attached picture - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - a cutaway of THREE rooms standing side by side in a row, separated by TWO thick walls, each wall pierced by one open doorway so a thing can pass from the left room to the middle room and from the middle room to the right room. Draw the three rooms clearly equal in size and clearly separated.

THE LEFT ROOM: a brass spyglass on a small stand, its front lens washed RUBY RED, tilted down over a bench. Gripped in its claw is one PALE, almost white, round droplet - it has picked up the pale one. On the bench beside it lies one DARK, nearly black, round droplet, untouched and rolling away from the claw. Set into the outer left wall of this room is a narrow COIN SLOT with a single coin half way into it, dropping in.

THE MIDDLE ROOM: a wide wooden TUB brim full of water that is FOAMING with bubbles. A hand holds a long LADLE, lifting a scoop of the foaming water out of the tub and tipping it into a small floor drain.

THE RIGHT ROOM: a squat iron stove whose whole body is drawn as deep FOLDED PLEATS, like a bellows, with a chimney on top. One DARK, nearly black, round droplet is escaping upward out of the chimney and drifting away off the top right corner.

OUTSIDE the rooms, on the bare ground to the far right: a cloth SACK, slumped and clearly half empty, with a small hole low on its side and a few sugar grains spilling out of the hole onto the ground.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people beyond the single hand holding the ladle, no plants, no machinery.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of rooms other than three; the walls drawn without doorways, or the rooms drawn merged into one space; the spyglass gripping the DARK droplet - it must grip the PALE one, and the DARK one must lie loose on the bench; the front lens not being ruby red; the coin slot missing or the coin drawn beside the slot instead of dropping into it; the tub drawn without foam or bubbles; the ladle missing, or the ladle pouring into the tub instead of out of it; the stove drawn smooth instead of deeply pleated; the droplet leaving the chimney being pale instead of dark; the sack drawn plump and full, or drawn without the hole and the spilled grains; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
