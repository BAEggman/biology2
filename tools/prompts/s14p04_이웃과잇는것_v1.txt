Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens and a little brick red, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - on the LEFT two thirds of the picture: TWO tall rooms standing side by side and sharing one common wall between them, seen from the front with their front walls cut away so you can see inside both. Their shared middle wall runs from top to bottom, and THREE different things join the two rooms through that shared wall, at three clearly separated heights.

AT THE TOP of the shared wall: one continuous BAND runs across the join, stitched shut with a neat row of stitches like a sewn seam, going right around without a single break. ABOVE that band, resting on top of it, sits a shallow POOL of water that is plainly held in - not one drop leaks past the band, and the wall below the band is completely dry.

AT THE MIDDLE of the shared wall: THREE stout RIVETS pass straight through the shared wall, pinning the two rooms together. From each end of every rivet, a thick WAVY CRINKLED CORD runs away into the room behind it, and all those cords converge on one ROUND LUMP sitting in the middle of each room, wrapping it and plainly holding it in place.

AT THE BOTTOM of the shared wall: FOUR short bare PIPES pass through the shared wall side by side. Small round BEADS are travelling through these pipes from one room to the other. The pipes are plainly bare metal tubes - no sleeve, no covering, nothing wrapped around them.

ON THE RIGHT THIRD of the picture, set clearly apart: TWO smaller rooms with thick stiff straight walls, also sharing one wall. Through that shared wall runs ONE wide TUNNEL, and the inside surface of the tunnel is papered in the SAME patterned wallpaper as the two rooms' inner walls, running through unbroken so the two rooms' insides are plainly one continuous space. Passing through that tunnel, going from one room to the other, are a ball of TANGLED THREAD and a FOLDED SHEET OF PAPER, as well as a few small round beads.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no animals, no furniture, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the two left rooms not sharing a wall, or the three joins not being clearly separated top, middle and bottom; the stitched band being drawn broken or interrupted anywhere; water leaking past the band or appearing below it; any number of rivets other than three; a rivet with no wavy cord attached, or the cords not reaching the round lump; any number of bottom pipes other than four; the bottom pipes being drawn wrapped, sleeved or covered instead of bare; the right pair of rooms having a stitched band, rivets or bare pipes; the right tunnel's inside being drawn bare instead of papered continuously; the thread ball or folded paper appearing in the left rooms' pipes instead of the right tunnel; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
