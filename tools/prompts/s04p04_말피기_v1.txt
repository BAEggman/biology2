One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE SETTING: A shop wall holding FOUR square display alcoves in a two-by-two grid, each a shallow pale niche with a plain floor. A suit of armour or a creature stands in each. Every piece of armour in the shop is covered all over in OVERLAPPING SCALES like a lobster's shell, and every arm and leg joint is pinned with a small dark iron HINGE. / TOP LEFT ALCOVE: A suit of armour whose helmet carries FOUR feelers - TWO curving up over the head and TWO more sticking out sideways across the front of the face. The armour stands with both feet in a round wooden TUB of water. / TOP RIGHT ALCOVE: A suit of armour whose helmet carries only TWO feelers, both curving up over the head, and nothing at all in front of the face. It stands on dry ground. / BOTTOM LEFT ALCOVE - AND THE ONE NEW THING: A suit of armour whose helmet is completely SMOOTH AND BARE on top with NO feelers of any kind, and which instead has a pair of sharp curved FANGS jutting down in front of its mouth. From the waist of that armour hangs a bundle of fine BRASS-COLOURED TUBES, long and slack, exactly like a horse's tail hanging down. The loose lower ends of that bundle DIP INTO a shallow round pool of dark RED liquid on the alcove floor, so the tube-ends are plainly soaking in the red pool. Only this alcove has the tube bundle and only this alcove has the red pool. / BOTTOM RIGHT ALCOVE: Two long many-segmented creatures. The UPPER one has legs packed DENSELY along its whole body, so close together they almost touch, and it holds a sprig of green leaves in its mouth. The LOWER one has legs set FAR APART with clear gaps between them, and it grips a red piece of meat in its mouth with a pair of claws that have a dark venom drop on the tip. / WHAT MUST NOT BE IN THE PICTURE: writing, letters or numbers; feelers on the bottom-left helmet; a red pool in any alcove but the bottom left; a fifth alcove. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: top left has four feelers and stands in a tub of water; top right has exactly two feelers and dry ground; bottom left has no feelers, has fangs, and its hanging bundle of fine brass tubes dips its ends into a shallow red pool; bottom right has one densely legged creature eating leaves and one sparsely legged creature with venom claws eating meat.
