A worker in yellow dungarees stands below and hooks a long pole into the bottom rail of the shutter and DRAGS IT DOWNWARD. The shutter is almost all the way DOWN - it covers nearly the whole slit and only a hair-thin dark gap is left at the very bottom. It is plainly being SHUT, not opened. There is exactly ONE shutter in the picture and it is nearly closed. / 2.
