the scene

The square is divided down the middle by one thin vertical rule into a LEFT half and a RIGHT half. Each half shows the front of the same small theatre: a plain doorway with an open door and a signboard above it reading THEATRE. Above each doorway, near the top of its half, one wide ribbon floats.

the left half

- the ribbon at the top is dusty teal. It loops over itself once into a neat knot and then runs on to the right edge in one unbroken piece, its end square and whole.
- one uniformed doorman in a teal suit and peaked cap leans against the wall to the left of the doorway with his arms folded. He is not blocking anything. The doorway beside him is wide open.
- a queue of ordinary people holding tickets walks in through that open door.

the right half

- the ribbon at the top is warm gold. It loops over itself once into a knot and then its end is TORN OFF in a ragged zigzag partway along, so it plainly stops short.

- the same uniformed doorman has taken the board with the pot on it and now sits squarely down on a low step right in the middle of the doorway, board and pot resting on his knees, filling the opening with his body.
- a queue of ordinary people holding tickets stands waiting outside, unable to get in.
