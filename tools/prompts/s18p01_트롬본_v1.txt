Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add ONE new thing. THE ADDITION - a brass TROMBONE. Lay it flat on the bare rock ledge at the feet of the BEARDED WORKMAN IN THE GREEN HELMET on the right, the one whose forearm is gripped by the bald man in the grey jumper and who carries a bucket in his other hand. It lies on the stone just below his boots, touching nobody and nothing else. Draw it clearly as a trombone seen from the side: one long straight brass tube with a wide CONE-SHAPED BELL flaring open at one end, and a long U-SHAPED SLIDE - two parallel tubes joined by a rounded U bend - running out from the other end. It is polished brass, warm yellow-brown. It has NO valves, NO buttons, NO keys and NO finger holes anywhere on it. It is about as long as the workman's leg, big enough to notice at a glance. One trombone only, and it is the only musical instrument in the picture. Nothing else is added.
