[이자섬 — 섬 위의 네 집과 유리 수조 · 첫 프롬프트]

Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Slightly raised three-quarter view, like looking down on a model. Keep it uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - a small flat ISLAND sitting in calm water, with FOUR little huts standing in a ROW along the island, all facing the viewer, each with one open serving window. The four huts are DIFFERENT SIZES. From left to right they are: a MEDIUM hut, then the BIGGEST hut (clearly the largest of the four, about twice as wide as the medium one), then a SMALL hut, then a TINY hut at the far right end.

IN FRONT OF THE ISLAND, standing in the shallow water close to the viewer, is a large upright GLASS TANK with clear water in it. A single broad GREEN BAND is painted horizontally around the middle of the tank. Floating on the water inside the tank are many small round GRAPES, and the water surface sits inside that green band.

AT THE MEDIUM HUT (leftmost): a man leans out of the window swinging a heavy wooden CLUB, beating a bulging SACK of grapes that hangs beside him. Grapes knocked loose from the sack tumble down a wooden CHUTE that runs from his window into the top of the glass tank. A large black ARROW beside that chute points UP.

AT THE BIGGEST HUT (second from left): a person in a long WHITE COAT holds a SYRINGE in one raised hand, and with the other hand works a long-handled SCOOP that is lifting a load of grapes OUT of the glass tank and tipping them away to the right, off the island. A large black ARROW beside that scoop points DOWN.

AT THE SMALL HUT (third): a person stands in the window with BOTH PALMS held out flat and open, one turned toward the club man on the left and one turned toward the white-coated person, as if pressing both of them down. A short plain BATON is tucked through this person's belt.

AT THE TINY HUT (far right): no person - only a SHORT LENGTH OF CHAIN, a few links, hanging in the little open window.

Nothing else is in the picture. No other buildings, no boats, no trees, no crowd.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the four huts drawn the same size, or the biggest hut anywhere but SECOND from the left; the tiny hut not being the smallest; grapes drawn as bunches on vines instead of loose round grapes floating in the tank; the chute pouring OUT of the tank, or the scoop putting grapes IN - the left one must ADD and the coated one must REMOVE; the two arrows pointing the same way; the green band drawn as several stripes instead of ONE broad band, or the water level sitting far above or far below it; the palms-out person holding any tool in the hands - the hands must be empty and open, with the baton only in the belt; the white coat or the syringe missing; the club drawn as an axe, hammer or stick with a head; any writing, letters, numbers or labels appearing anywhere.
