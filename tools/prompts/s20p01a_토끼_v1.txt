Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add ONE new thing. THE ADDITION - a RABBIT. Put it on the bare soil floor in the open empty space in the lower middle of the picture, between the long wooden plank lying on the ground and the terracotta pot holding the half-yellow half-green leaf, sitting on the soil and touching nobody and nothing else. Draw it as a plain ordinary live rabbit seen from the side: a soft grey-brown body, two long ears standing straight up, a small round tail, sitting up on its hind legs with its front paws down on the soil, its head turned to look towards the man in the red overalls with the paintbrush. It is a real animal, about as tall as the terracotta pot beside it. One rabbit only, and it is the only animal in the picture. Nothing else is added. Everything else stays exactly identical: the tall central stalk with the ZIPPER running up it, still open at the top, and the small YELLOW GRAINS inside that opening still SCATTERED loosely and spread apart exactly as they are now - do not gather them, line them up, count them or move them, and do not let a single grain fall onto the soil; the man standing on the wooden STEPLADDER spraying water from a spray bottle, and the falling drops; the man kneeling at the left swinging a wooden MALLET at a cracked seed husk on the ground; the man at the lower left lifting the long wooden PLANK that rests on a grey stone, using it as a lever under the base of the stalk; the man in the RED OVERALLS at the lower right holding a paintbrush and a can of green paint, painting the leaf so that one half of it is green and the other half stays yellow; the small potted plant beside him with exactly ONE long side shoot growing out of it and its top uncut; the terracotta pot with the half-yellow half-green leaf; the glass greenhouse frame with its pale metal beams and roof; the bare soil floor; the same warm cream paper, the same thin hand-inked storybook linework and soft flat watercolour washes. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the yellow grains inside the zipper being clumped together, tidied, reduced, added to, or spilled onto the ground; a second rabbit or any other animal appearing; the rabbit being drawn as a hare with huge legs, a toy, a statue, a hat, a stuffed doll or a cartoon character - it is a plain live rabbit; the rabbit being held by anyone, standing on the plank, inside a pot, or up on the ladder; the rabbit eating the plants or touching the paint can; the potted plant gaining a second side shoot or having its top cut; the painted leaf becoming fully green or fully yellow; the paintbrush turning into a stiff scrubbing brush; the mallet, the seed husk, the stepladder, the spray bottle, the plank or the stone disappearing; any writing, letters or numbers appearing anywhere.
