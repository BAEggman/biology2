A small factory town by a river. On the LEFT stands a red-brick FACTORY with its front wall cut away so we see inside. On the RIGHT stands a round pink FACTORY. High over the river, a long stone BRIDGE connects the left side to the right side.

2. In that big room, two NANNY workers in aprons feed the little swimmers with baby bottles. One nanny holds up a red STOP paddle toward a pipe coming down from the sky, down which a light-brown egg standing in an egg cup is sliding.
3. In a SMALL side room of the same factory, a worker assembles shiny TIN ROBOTS; some robots march into the big room carrying wrenches to help, and one robot wears a big fake mustache.
4. From the bottom of the left factory a pipe comes out: first into a coiled storage drum, then a long pipe. Three side tanks pour into the long pipe: a tank shaped like a sugar sack, a tank shaped like a chestnut, and a tiny pea-sized bulb. The long pipe ends in an outlet spout.
5. In the left factory's front yard, a small baby-sized building has two little pipes: a worker in a hard hat smashes the pipe painted with a windmill with a sledgehammer, while a tin robot hugs and holds up the pipe painted with a WOLF.

10. Next to the starfish, a father and a mother shuffle colored cards together; their four children all wear shirts of different colors; one mosquito can bite only one child.
11. In the river, two fish release clouds of tiny eggs into the water; a snail wears both a necktie and a hair bow; one fish is half pink and half blue.
