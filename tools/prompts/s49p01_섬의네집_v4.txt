Keep this exact picture and this exact style. Every hut, every person, the club and sack, the chute, the syringe, the white clothes, the baton on the third sill, the chain, the glass tank, the green band, the grapes and both arrows all stay exactly as they are. Add only THREE small objects, one to each of the first three huts.

FIRST HUT (the leftmost one, the man with the club): place ONE smooth WHITE EGG, about the size of a fist, resting on the window sill at the left end of that sill, clear of the chute.

SECOND HUT (the big middle one, the person in white with the syringe): place ONE neatly FOLDED WHITE CLOTH, a small square folded pile of plain white linen, resting on the window sill at the left end of that sill.

THIRD HUT (the person holding both palms open): put a TRIANGULAR HAT on that person's head - a plain three-cornered cone hat with straight sides, clearly a triangle seen from the front. The hands stay open and empty, the baton stays lying on the sill.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the egg drawn as a ball, stone, pearl or grape - it must read as a smooth egg; the folded cloth drawn as a book, box, paper or towel on a rail - it is a flat folded square pile of white linen; the triangular hat drawn as a round hat, a cap with a brim, a helmet or a crown - it must be a plain triangle; any of the three objects going to the wrong hut; the fourth hut changing at all; anything else in the picture moving or changing; any writing, letters or numbers appearing.
