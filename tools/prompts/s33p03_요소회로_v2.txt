One square illustration, hand-inked storybook style: thin brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, warm cream paper, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE SETTING: An industrial yard seen from above at an angle. A long RED BRICK WALL runs diagonally from the lower left to the upper right and splits the yard in two. BEYOND the wall runs a narrow CANAL with boats on it; call that whole strip THE CANAL SIDE. IN FRONT of the wall is open ground; call that THE YARD SIDE. The wall is pierced by two ARCHED TUNNELS, one toward the left and one toward the right. / ON THE CANAL SIDE (beyond the wall): One wooden boat heaped with thick iron CHAIN crosses the canal, the chain lying LOOSE in it, not tied or fastened. A second identical boat is moored and completely EMPTY. Three workmen in flat caps CHOP the chain into many SHORT EQUAL PIECES with hammers and chisels, and the stubs scatter around them. Beside them one square wooden CRATE lies TIPPED OVER on its side, mouth toward the viewer, completely EMPTY. Further right a workman pushes a rail CART heaped with fine dark GRANULES toward a grey stone DOMED building, and a bold straight ARROW points from the cart INTO its open doorway. / THE CRANK GOES ON THE CANAL SIDE TOO - THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING IN THE PICTURE: Standing on the canal side, RIGHT NEXT TO THE THREE CHAIN-CHOPPERS and on the same side of the brick wall as the boats, a workman turns a large iron hand CRANK mounted on a stone pedestal. Beside him a second man PINCHES HIS NOSE shut against a stench and a cluster of small DARK BUBBLES rises from a vent at his feet. All three - crank, nose-pinching man, bubbles - are BEYOND the wall, in among the boats and the choppers. None of them may stand on the yard side. / THE RING OF CARTS CROSSES THE WALL TWICE: One closed OVAL RAIL TRACK carries four small carts around a single loop, and that loop STRADDLES the wall - the rails run out of the left archway, curve around the open yard, and run back in through the right archway. Two carts stand on the yard side in the open, and the other two are up on the canal side beside the crank, so the crank is plainly the start of the loop. Draw the rails clearly entering both archways. / ON THE YARD SIDE, at the far end of the loop, a folded QUILT is unrolled and SPREAD FLAT on the open ground beside the track, and past it an emptied cart runs back toward the right archway. / THE BARRIER: On the yard side at the lower right, a red-and-white striped BARRIER ARM is lowered and its tip rests squarely ACROSS a long unbroken iron CHAIN lying on the ground and heading for the domed building. A bold CURVED ARROW beside that chain bends BACK the way it came, so the chain is plainly turned back and never reaches the building. The granule cart passes freely; only the chain is stopped. / MUST NOT APPEAR: lettering, numbers, signs, a second barrier, a broom. The crank must never be on the yard side. / WHEN DONE: the crank, the nose-pinching man and the dark bubbles all stand BEYOND the wall among the boats and chain-choppers, never on the open yard. The oval rail loop runs out of one archway and back in through the other, with two carts on each side. A quilt lies spread flat on the yard side. The barrier rests across the long chain with a curved arrow turning it back, while the granule cart rolls into the domed building.
