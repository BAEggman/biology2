the layout

One single tall teal-green post stands straight down the middle of the square and divides it into a LEFT half and a RIGHT half. On the LEFT half a thin horizontal ink line divides that half again into an upper scene and a lower scene. The RIGHT half is one single scene, not divided.

left half, upper scene

A bearded man in a teal jumper and mustard trousers faces right, pushing one round-ringed key into a keyhole plate. That plate is bolted ONTO a heavy iron-barred gate — plate and gate are one piece of ironwork. The gate has swung open and a stream of small grey beads pours out through the opening. Rising up just past the open gate is one large bare human arm bent at the elbow, fist up, the biceps bulging.

left half, lower scene

The same man, the same iron gate with the plate bolted onto it, the same open gate, the same grey beads. But here there is NO arm and no muscle of any kind. Instead, beyond the open gate stands a SECOND man in a grey shirt lifting a second round-ringed key towards a second keyhole plate on a second iron gate — one man passing the same act to the next.

right half

Flat on the bare wall hangs one small keyhole plate on its own — no gate, no door, no bars, no hinges, nothing attached. A bearded man in a teal jumper holds a round-ringed key up to it.

From that lone plate a wide pale corridor runs off to the right and FORKS into exactly two branches: one branch climbs to the upper right, the other drops to the lower right. Along the corridor runs one man in a red vest, carrying one round flat disc in his hand.

At the end of the upper branch is a small room. A pendulum hangs on a cord from that room's ceiling, and a man in blue-grey overalls has caught the pendulum's round bob in his hand and holds it still.

At the end of the lower branch is a second small room, where one hand grips the narrow neck of a soft water bag and squeezes it so three drops fall from its bottom.
