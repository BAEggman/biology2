Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - TWO square rooms standing side by side on bare ground, seen from the front with the front wall cut away so you can see inside. A clear gap separates them. They are the same size but they are being divided in two completely different ways.

THE LEFT ROOM has soft cloth-like walls. A single thin TWISTED CORD is wrapped once around the OUTSIDE of the room at its middle height, encircling it like a belt, and it has been pulled so tight that the room's walls are squeezed inward into a deep narrow WAIST - the room is plainly being pinched in two from the outside. THREE small people hang from that cord on the outside, gripping it with both hands and hauling on it, leaning back with their feet braced against the wall. The inside of the left room is empty.

THE RIGHT ROOM has stiff straight walls that are not squeezed at all - they stay perfectly square. Across the middle of its floor, a row of flat wooden PLANKS is being laid, running from the left wall toward the right wall; the row is only about HALF finished, so there is a plain gap of bare floor where the remaining planks have yet to go. Standing upright on either side of that unfinished row are TWO short lengths of green PLASTIC MESH FENCING, one on each side, marking out the line the planks must follow. Two small hand CARTS, one entering from the left wall and one from the right wall, each carry a stack of loose planks toward the unfinished gap. There is no cord anywhere around this room and its walls are not pinched.

Nothing else is in the picture. No animals, no tools other than the two carts, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the right room's walls being pinched or waisted - only the LEFT room is squeezed; a cord appearing around the right room; the left room having planks laid across it; the plank row in the right room being drawn complete instead of half finished; any number of plastic mesh fences other than two, or the fences lying flat instead of standing upright; any number of carts other than two, or both carts entering from the same side; any number of people other than three, or the people standing inside the rooms instead of hanging on the cord outside the left room; the encircling cord being drawn as a thick rope or a chain instead of a thin twisted cord; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
