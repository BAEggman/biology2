Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Slightly raised three-quarter view, like looking down on a model. Keep it readable, not crowded.

WHAT TO DRAW - a squat RED BRICK BUILDING standing on bare ground, its whole front wall cut away so the viewer sees straight inside. It has a plain flat roof and one wide doorway on the right side.

INSIDE THE BUILDING: one big woody VINE grows from a single thick stem and splits again and again into MANY BRANCHING ARMS, filling the room like a tree of branches, and small round GRAPES hang all over those branches, still attached.

AT THE DOORWAY, INSIDE: a man swings a heavy wooden CLUB at the branches. Grapes knocked loose from the branches roll along the floor and out through the doorway, spilling onto the ground outside.

JUST OUTSIDE THE DOORWAY: a person in long WHITE CLOTHES holding a SYRINGE raised in one hand is picking loose grapes up off the ground with the other hand and reaching back inside to hang them onto the branches again. Show clearly that grapes go BOTH ways at this doorway - some rolling out, some being carried back in.

AT THE LEFT SIDE OF THE BUILDING, OUTSIDE, stands a low WORK TABLE. Two porters walk up to it, one carrying a bulging SACK on his shoulder and one carrying a fat OIL JUG. On the table someone is shaping NEW GRAPES out of what they bring, and a small line of those new grapes runs from the table in through a side opening toward the vine.

BEHIND THE BUILDING, to the right: a small handcart is being pulled away, and lying FLAT and OPEN on it - not rolled, not folded - is one checkered quilted MAT.

FAR RIGHT, well apart from everything else, stands a small stone building with a DOMED roof and a wide opening that has NO DOOR AT ALL in it. A few loose grapes roll freely in through that open mouth.

Nothing else is in the picture. No other buildings, no trees, no animals, no crowd, no water.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the big building drawn in stone, wood or plaster instead of RED BRICK; the vine drawn as a single straight stalk or as one bunch of grapes - it must branch again and again; the grapes on the branches drawn as tight clusters hanging like fruit at a market - they should read as many single round grapes attached along the branches; the club man standing outside instead of inside; the white-clothed person hitting anything, or carrying grapes away instead of putting them back; the work table having no sack and no oil jug beside it; the mat on the cart being rolled up or folded into a pile - it must lie flat and open; the domed building having a door, a gate, shutters or a turnstile in its opening; any writing, letters, numbers or labels appearing anywhere.
