One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No written words and no numbers anywhere in the picture.

the scene

One tall building seen from the side with its front wall removed, so all its floors are open like a doll's house. It has three floors above ground with warm sandy walls, and one grey basement below ground. A grey ground line runs across at basement ceiling height.

the pipework on the left, outside the building

- one thick pipe that coils and loops lazily runs down the outside and feeds a soft rubber hose into each of the three upper floors. Its far end frays into loose strands.
- one separate straight rod runs down past all three upper floors without stopping at any of them, and goes on down into the basement. It is straight and stiff, quite unlike the coiling pipe.

what is on each floor, from the top down

1. top floor. One workman in green holds the coiled hose. Beside him stands one tall glass salt shaker with a perforated cap. The shaker has tipped and a scatter of small WHITE grains has spilled onto the floor, and the workman is sweeping those grains back up into the shaker with a small brush — he is putting them back, not pouring them out.

2. middle floor. One workman holds the coiled hose. Beside him stands one round layer cake on a cake stand. Standing next to the cake, as tall as the cake itself, is one large pale CORK STOPPER — a plain tapered cork, wider at the top, exactly the shape of a cork pulled from a bottle.

3. third floor. One workman holds the coiled hose. Beside him stands one large red heart. Standing next to the heart is one small TIN TOY ROBOT — square head, square body, jointed tube arms and legs, a small antenna on its head.

4. basement, below the ground line. One workman braces himself and hauls hard on the straight rod. In front of him stand four metal drums in a row; their lids have flown off and pale spray bursts up out of all of them at once.

on the roof

One round-faced clock standing on legs, and beside it, up in the sky, one plain sun with straight rays. No wire or pipe of any kind reaches the clock.

what must be true when you are done

- the salt grains are WHITE and are being swept back INTO the shaker, not poured out
- the cork stopper on the middle floor is unmistakably a bottle cork, tall and tapered, and it is not a cake, not a candle and not a pot
- the tin toy robot on the third floor is unmistakably a robot — square head and body with an antenna — and stands beside the heart
- the coiling pipe feeds all three upper floors and stops there; the straight rod passes them all and reaches only the basement
- all four drums are open and spraying at the same moment
- nothing at all connects to the clock on the roof
- one building, one square scene, not a grid and not split into panels
