Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add ONE new thing. THE ADDITION - a MELON. Put it on the flat top edge of the ROOF, in the empty stretch of roof to the LEFT of the round CLOCK that stands up there, sitting on the roof line and touching nothing else. Draw it as one whole uncut musk melon seen from the side: a round pale green fruit with a raised net of fine cream-coloured veins all over its skin and a short dry stalk on top. It is about the same size across as the clock face. It is whole - not cut, not sliced, not opened, with nothing spilling out of it. One melon only, and it is the only fruit in the picture. Nothing else is added. Everything else stays exactly identical, and the counted things matter most - do not add, remove, move or re-count a single one of them: the round CLOCK on the roof, standing on its two small feet with NO cable, NO wire and NO rope of any kind touching it - it must stay completely unconnected; the SUN drawn in the sky at the top right with its straight rays; the tall open cut-away building showing exactly FOUR floors, no more and no fewer; the TOP floor with the man holding a coil of hose, the BROOM sweeping scattered white grains, and the big tipped-over SALT SHAKER pouring grains out of it; the SECOND floor with the man holding a coil of hose, the round layered CAKE on its white stand, and the tall CORK stopper standing beside it; the THIRD floor with the man holding a coil of hose, the big RED HEART, and the little TIN ROBOT with its square head and antenna; the BOTTOM floor, drawn in grey, with the man hauling on the straight rod and exactly FOUR metal DRUMS whose lids are blowing off in a burst; the long LOOPING COILED HOSE hanging down the outside on the left with its frayed end; the straight thick grey PIPE that runs down the outside and into every floor; the grey ground band across the lower part of the picture; the same warm cream paper, the same thin hand-inked storybook linework and soft flat watercolour washes. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the melon being drawn as a ball, a balloon, a stone, a cabbage, a pumpkin, an apple, a cucumber, an egg or a globe - it is a netted musk melon with a stalk; the melon being cut open, sliced, or having seeds showing; a second melon or any other fruit or vegetable appearing anywhere; the melon being placed inside the building, on any floor, in anyone's hands, or beside the cake; the melon touching, covering or hiding the clock; the clock gaining a cable, a wire or a rope; the sun disappearing, moving or being covered; the number of drums changing from four; the number of floors changing from four; the salt shaker, the broom, the cake, the cork, the heart or the tin robot disappearing; the coiled hose losing its loops or its frayed end; any writing, letters or numbers appearing anywhere.
