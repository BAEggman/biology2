One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No written words and no numbers anywhere in the picture.

the scene

A plain workbench seen straight on. On it sits one large round ball of dough, cut clean in half so the flat cut face is turned towards the viewer. Behind the bench is a plain shelf.

what is in it

1. the big ball, centre-left. Its cut face shows three layers, one inside the other, like the rings of an onion — the outermost layer is dusty blue, the middle layer is dull red, the innermost layer is pale yellow. The three layers are clearly separate bands of even thickness and easy to tell apart.

2. the ball's right side has been pushed in with a thumb, so a deep round tunnel runs from that dent into the middle of the ball. The mouth of the tunnel is a plain round opening on the outside of the ball. The whole inner wall of the tunnel is lined in the same pale yellow as the innermost layer.

3. two small pouches bulge outward from the wall of that tunnel, side by side, each joined to the tunnel by a short neck. The upper pouch is shaped like a pair of soft leaves. The lower pouch is one heavy smooth sack that sags with its own weight.

4. three ribbons run from the cut face across to the shelf, one from each layer, and each ribbon is the colour of the layer it comes from.
- the dusty blue ribbon goes to two things on the shelf: one flat square patch of tanned leather, and one long open-ended tube lying beside it with a small rounded knob at its near end.
- the dull red ribbon goes to two things: one bare human arm bent at the elbow with the muscle bunched hard, and behind it one open crate spilling over with many different small tools.
- the pale yellow ribbon does not go to the shelf at all: it runs back into the ball and follows the lining of the tunnel.

5. far left on the bench, well apart from the big ball. One much smaller ball of dough, also cut in half. Its cut face shows only two layers — a dusty blue outer and a pale yellow inner, touching each other directly. There is no red layer in this small ball at all.

what must be true when you are done

- the big ball's cut face shows exactly three layers and the small ball's cut face shows exactly two
- the small ball has no red layer anywhere, and the gap where a red layer would be is simply not there
- the tunnel's lining is the same pale yellow as the innermost layer, so they read as one continuous surface
- there are exactly two pouches on the tunnel wall — one leafy, one a heavy sagging sack
- exactly three ribbons leave the cut face, one per layer, each matching its layer's colour
- the blue ribbon reaches the leather patch and the open-ended tube; the red ribbon reaches the flexed arm and the tool crate
- one bench, one square scene, not a grid and not split into panels
- warm cream paper background, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind
