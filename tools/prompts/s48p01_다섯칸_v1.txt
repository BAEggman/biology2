Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about two and a half times as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - one long shallow wooden CASE lying flat, seen from above, divided by thin wooden walls into exactly FIVE equal square compartments in a row from left to right. Draw the dividing walls clearly so the five compartments are unmistakable and easy to count. Each compartment contains a small round TENT and some short thick RODS and some ROPES, in the states described below. Nothing else is in any compartment.

COMPARTMENT 1 (far left): a small round TENT stands upright in the middle of the compartment, but its fabric sags and one side has torn open. Inside the tent, seen through the torn side, lie TWO short thick RODS. Two ROPES lie in the compartment, one at the left wall and one at the right wall, each with only its frayed END showing - neither rope reaches the tent.

COMPARTMENT 2: the same tent has COLLAPSED completely and lies flat on the floor as a crumpled heap of fabric pushed to the corners. The TWO rods now lie loose and scattered on the floor, at different angles. The two ropes have come in from the side walls and each rope END now TOUCHES one of the rods.

COMPARTMENT 3: no tent at all - only a few scraps of fabric swept against the compartment's outer rim. The TWO rods now stand side by side in ONE NEAT VERTICAL LINE down the exact middle of the compartment, and a THIN DASHED LINE is drawn down the middle along that row. The two ropes are pulled straight and TAUT from the left and right walls to the rods.

COMPARTMENT 4: no tent, no fabric. There are now FOUR rods: two pushed hard against the LEFT wall and two pushed hard against the RIGHT wall, with a wide empty gap in the middle. The ropes are now SHORT and coiled up tight near the walls.

COMPARTMENT 5 (far right): TWO small round tents stand newly pitched, one near the left wall and one near the right wall, both whole and neat with no tears. Inside each tent lies a loose tangle of THIN THREAD instead of rods. There are NO ropes at all in this compartment. The wooden floor of this compartment alone is PINCHED IN from both sides at its middle, so the compartment is visibly waisted like an hourglass.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no animals, no tools, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of compartments other than five; the dividing walls drawn so faintly the five compartments cannot be counted; a tent standing upright in compartments 2, 3 or 4; more or fewer than two rods in compartments 1, 2 and 3; any number of rods in compartment 4 other than four, or the four rods not split two and two against the two walls; the rods in compartment 3 lying scattered instead of standing in one neat middle row; the dashed line appearing in any compartment other than the third; ropes reaching the rods in compartment 1 - there the ropes must plainly fall short; any rope appearing in compartment 5; rods appearing inside the tents of compartment 5 - those tents hold loose thin thread; more or fewer than two tents in compartment 5, or those tents drawn torn or sagging; the pinched waisted floor appearing in any compartment other than the fifth; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
