Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - one bundle of dry straw lying flat across the MIDDLE of the picture, pinched narrow at its middle, with one small metal PLATE bolted on top of that narrow middle and one iron HOOK on the plate.

THE TWO STARS: at the far LEFT and the far RIGHT of the picture there is one STAR-SHAPED lump each - a solid rounded core with many straight spokes radiating out of it all around, like a child's drawing of a star burst. The two stars are the same size and face each other across the straw bundle.

INSIDE EACH STAR: set into the very centre of each star are TWO short round DISCS, each disc drawn as a ring with a plainly EMPTY HOLE through its middle, and the two discs are set at RIGHT ANGLES to each other - one lying flat, the other standing on edge, forming a T. Exactly two discs in each star, no more.

THE THREE KINDS OF SPOKE - draw each star's spokes so three kinds can be told apart:
  - ONE spoke from each star runs to the straw bundle and is caught on its HOOK;
  - SEVERAL long spokes from the LEFT star run toward the right and SEVERAL long spokes from the RIGHT star run toward the left, and in the middle of the picture, ABOVE the straw bundle, these two sets OVERLAP and lie alongside one another for a stretch, sliding past each other like two combs pushed together;
  - the remaining spokes are SHORT and point outward, away from the middle, toward the picture's edges.

IN THE BOTTOM RIGHT CORNER, well away from everything else and much smaller, draw one small square ROOM with stiff straight walls, seen from the front with its front wall cut away. Inside that little room there are spokes reaching in from its left and right walls toward a small straw bundle - but there is NO STAR anywhere in that room, and no discs. The spokes there simply come out of the bare wall.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no animals, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of big stars other than two; a star drawn as a flat five-pointed cartoon star instead of a round core with many radiating spokes; any number of discs inside a star other than two; the two discs drawn parallel or stacked instead of at right angles to each other; a disc drawn solid instead of a ring with an empty hole; the two sets of long spokes merely touching end to end instead of clearly overlapping and lying alongside each other; the overlap happening anywhere other than the middle of the picture above the straw bundle; more than one spoke per star reaching the hook; a star or any disc appearing inside the small room in the bottom right corner; the small room being drawn the same size as the main scene; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
