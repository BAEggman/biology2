One WIDE landscape illustration, about twice as wide as it is tall, in a soft hand-drawn diagram style: thin pencil-brown outlines, pale watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere.

the layout

One thin vertical line splits the picture into a LEFT half and a RIGHT half. Each half shows the same thing in a different state: a pair of kidney-shaped cells sitting in a horizontal surface, with a wide flat chamber drawn underneath them like a room cut open.

left half — open

The two kidney-shaped cells are plump and swollen, pale green inside with rosy outer rims, and they bow apart so the gap between them stands wide open.

Above them, slanting blue light rays come down from the upper left and upper right. Set into the top of each cell is one small round BLUE GLASS BEAD catching that light. Hanging in the air to the right of the cells is one round POCKET WATCH on a short chain.

Through the open gap, small DARK grey droplets travel downward into the chamber, and small PALE cream droplets travel upward out of it. The chamber below holds a mixture: plenty of dark droplets and plenty of pale ones.

right half — shut

The same two kidney-shaped cells, but now thin and shrivelled with tan-gold rims, pressed together so the gap between them is closed tight. Above them the ground is cracked dry earth and a few curling wind lines blow across — no light rays, no blue beads, no watch.

The chamber below is packed with PALE cream droplets only. There is not one dark droplet anywhere in that right-hand chamber.

★ the one new thing — inside the right chamber

Standing on the floor of that right-hand chamber, among the pale droplets, is one small BRASS SPYGLASS — a short telescope on a folding tripod. Its front lens is a deep glowing RUBY RED disc, the only strongly red thing in the whole picture. The spyglass is tipped so its ruby lens faces one pale cream droplet close in front of it, and that droplet is already half drawn into the mouth of the tube.

There is no spyglass in the left half.

what must be true when you are done

- the left cells are swollen and apart; the right cells are shrivelled and shut
- the blue beads and the pocket watch appear in the LEFT half only
- the cracked earth and wind lines appear in the RIGHT half only
- the left chamber holds both dark and pale droplets; the right chamber holds pale droplets ONLY, not one dark droplet
- exactly one brass spyglass, in the RIGHT chamber, its front lens a deep ruby red
- the spyglass is taking in a PALE droplet, not a dark one
- a wide landscape picture about twice as wide as tall, warm cream paper, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind
