Draw another picture in exactly the same style as the one you just made - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image.

WHAT TO DRAW - in the middle of the page, the same square four-room building as before, small and simple: cut open at the front, divided by one floor and one wall into exactly FOUR rooms, TWO small ones on top and TWO large ones below, with the lower LEFT room's walls drawn much thicker than the rest. Keep the building compact so there is room around it.

TWO PIPE LOOPS - draw two big rounded PIPE loops that leave the building and come back to it, so that together they read as a lying-down figure eight with the building at the waist.

THE LEFT-HAND LOOP: a pipe leaves the LOWER RIGHT room through its outer wall, curves out to the LEFT of the building, passes through a pair of soft rounded LUNG-SHAPED bags drawn as two plump sacs joined at the top, and comes back into the UPPER LEFT room. Colour the pipe leaving the building a dull grey-blue and the pipe returning from the sacs a soft brick red.

THE RIGHT-HAND LOOP: a pipe leaves the LOWER LEFT room through its outer wall, curves out to the RIGHT of the building, passes around a simple rounded TORSO shape - a plain headless body outline, arms and legs suggested with a few strokes - and comes back into the UPPER RIGHT room. Colour the pipe leaving the building a soft brick red and the pipe returning from the torso a dull grey-blue.

Nothing else is in the picture.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the two loops crossing over each other or tangling - the left loop stays on the left and the right loop stays on the right; a loop starting or ending in the wrong room - LOWER RIGHT goes out to the sacs and comes back to UPPER LEFT, LOWER LEFT goes out to the torso and comes back to UPPER RIGHT; the pipe colours being swapped on a loop; the building having any number of rooms other than four; the lung sacs being drawn as balloons, clouds, bellows or bags of flour - they are two plump rounded sacs joined at the top; the torso being given a head or a face; any person, animal, valve, machine or heart shape appearing; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
