the scene

- the outermost ring is dusty blue, the next ring in is dull red, the innermost ring is pale yellow. All three are THIN BANDS of about the same thickness, like three coats of paint. None of them is a filled disc.
- inside the pale yellow ring the middle of the ball is EMPTY — plain cream paper, no dough at all. It is a hollow cavity.
- on the right side, the three rings all bend inwards together and run side by side down a narrow neck that opens on the outside of the ball, so the hollow middle is joined to the outside world by that one short neck. The neck is narrow — about a fifth of the width of the ball.
