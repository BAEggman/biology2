WHAT TO DRAW - a big round wooden TURNTABLE, like a lazy-susan workbench, seen at a slight angle from above so its whole top is visible, standing on plain cream ground. Three workmen in plain tunics and trousers stand around it, one at the RIGHT, one at the FRONT, one at the LEFT. On and around the turntable are wooden STICKS divided into square SEGMENTS like a chocolate bar, and small brass DRAWING PINS stuck into the ends of those sticks.

AT THE RIGHT of the turntable: the workman holds up a BRASS SPYGLASS whose front lens glows deep RUBY RED. With his other hand he is pressing a small DARK GREY DROPLET onto the end of a stick that has exactly FIVE segments and one brass pin at each of its two ends. Floating in the air just above him are exactly THREE dark grey droplets in total, including the one he is holding - no more and no fewer. Just past his hands, the joined stick has already snapped in half into TWO sticks of exactly THREE segments each, and they are falling onto the turntable.

ON THE TURNTABLE TOP: exactly SIX three-segment sticks lie spread around the wooden top. Count them carefully - six, no more and no fewer. They all look alike.

AT THE FRONT of the turntable: the second workman presses a flat round COIN into the top of a three-segment stick so that the stick now shows TWO brass pins, and beside him he holds up a small round wooden SHIELD toward another three-segment stick, which now shows only ONE pin again and has two or three short sparkle lines beside it to show it has been polished bright. Draw exactly ONE coin and ONE shield in his hands.

AT THE LEFT of the turntable: a short wooden CHUTE leads off the turntable to the outside, and exactly ONE three-segment stick is sliding down that chute away from the table. The third workman stands beside the chute and with both arms sweeps the other FIVE three-segment sticks back inward across the table, and where they gather they are being fitted together into one fresh FIVE-segment stick again. Beside his feet lies exactly ONE more coin on the ground.
