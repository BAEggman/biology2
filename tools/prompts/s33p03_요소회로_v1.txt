One square illustration, hand-inked storybook style: thin brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, warm cream paper, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE SETTING: An industrial yard. A long RED BRICK WALL runs diagonally across the picture from the lower left to the upper right and divides the yard into a FAR SIDE (beyond the wall) and a NEAR SIDE (in front of the wall). A narrow CANAL runs alongside the wall on the far side. The wall has two ARCHED TUNNELS through it, one left and one right. / 1. THE BOATS ON THE CANAL: One wooden boat carries a heavy load of thick iron CHAIN heaped in it and is crossing the canal; the chain simply LIES LOOSE in the boat - it is not tied, welded or fastened to the boat in any way. On the far bank a second identical boat sits moored and completely EMPTY. / 2. THE CHAIN BEING CUT UP: On the far side of the wall, beyond the canal, three workmen in flat caps take the chain ashore and CHOP IT INTO MANY SHORT EQUAL PIECES with hammers and chisels; a scatter of identical short chain-stubs lies on the ground around them. / 3. THE OVERTURNED BIN: Near those workmen one square wooden CRATE lies TIPPED OVER on its side and is completely EMPTY, with only a few loose crumbs spilled beside it. / 4. THE CART OF GRANULES GOES INTO THE DOMED BUILDING: A workman pushes a small rail CART heaped with fine dark GRANULES along an upper path toward a grey stone building with a rounded DOME roof at the upper right; a bold straight ARROW points from the cart INTO the building's open doorway. The cart passes freely, nothing blocks it. / 5. THE BARRIER TURNS THE LONG CHAIN BACK: Lower right, a red-and-white striped BARRIER ARM is lowered and its tip rests squarely ACROSS a long unbroken iron CHAIN that runs along the ground toward the domed building. Beside that blocked chain a bold CURVED ARROW bends back the way the chain came, so the chain is plainly TURNED BACK and never reaches the building. Only the granule cart gets through; only the long chain is stopped. / 6. THE RING OF CARTS - THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART, AND IT MUST STRADDLE THE WALL: A single closed OVAL RAIL TRACK carries four small carts around one loop. That loop is NOT all on one side of the wall - it passes THROUGH BOTH ARCHED TUNNELS, so roughly HALF the oval lies on the FAR side of the brick wall and the other half lies on the NEAR side, and the rails visibly enter one arch and come out of the other. Draw the track disappearing into each archway so a viewer can see the loop crossing the wall TWICE. / ON THE FAR SIDE OF THE WALL, inside, stand the starting CRANK and the first two carts: a workman turns a large iron hand CRANK mounted on a stone pedestal right beside the track, and the two carts nearest him are on the far side with him. Beside that crank a second man PINCHES HIS NOSE shut against a stench, and a cluster of small DARK BUBBLES rises from a vent in the ground at his feet - both of these stand on the FAR side too, feeding the crank. / ON THE NEAR SIDE OF THE WALL are the other two carts. From the last near-side cart a folded QUILT is being unrolled and SPREAD FLAT on the ground, and past it the emptied cart runs back into the near archway to begin the loop again. / MUST NOT APPEAR: lettering, numbers, signs, a second barrier, a broom. The ring of carts must never sit entirely on one side of the wall. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: the oval rail loop passes through BOTH arches so half of it is behind the wall and half in front, with the crank, the nose-pinching man and the dark bubbles all on the FAR side and the quilt spread flat on the NEAR side. The barrier arm rests across the long chain and a curved arrow turns it back, while the granule cart passes into the domed building.
