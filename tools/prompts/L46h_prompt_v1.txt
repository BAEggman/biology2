A neighbourhood street seen from the front. Every little house has its FRONT WALL CUT AWAY so we can see its rooms inside; rooms on the left side hold slate-blue water, rooms on the right side hold crimson-red water.

Right next to it, a TALL apartment block with its front cut away: white bubbles drifting in at its outer windows only reach the outermost rooms; the inner rooms deep inside are dark and their tenants gasp; a red pipe from a pump in the basement climbs up and delivers white bubbles to every inner room.

MIDDLE — A ROW OF FOUR SMALL NEIGHBOUR HOUSES, each with its animal sitting on its roof:
2. FISH HOUSE (a fish on the roof): inside only TWO rooms — one small room on top and one big room below. ONE single hose loop: from the house up through the gill comb, out to the garden, and straight back to the house.
