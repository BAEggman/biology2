One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No written words anywhere in the picture.

the scene

Three long horizontal bead-chains stretch right across the picture, one above the other, with plenty of empty cream space between them. A fourth, lower area holds a shallow pan. Each chain is a row of beads joined by short links, and the beads come in four kinds:
- plain pale rings
- pale gold diamonds, and from the top of every diamond rise three small curling wisps, like the steam off a cup, so the diamonds plainly smell of something
- one small white rice-bowl heaped with white rice
- one small pole carrying a little pennant flag, and the flag is cut into the shape of a capital R
- plain grey cubes

what happens on each chain, from the top down

1. top chain. One workman in green dungarees stands on the chain holding open scissors, cutting the link on the LEFT side of a steaming gold diamond. In his other hand, held up so it is easy to see, is one glass bottle of dark fizzy cola with bubbles rising in it.

2. middle chain. A second workman in red dungarees holds open scissors, cutting the link on the RIGHT side of the rice-bowl bead; the R-flag bead sits right beside the rice bowl, and both are on his left. In his other hand he holds up one tall enamel drip-coffee pot with a long thin curved spout.

3. lower chain. A third workman is dressed in a patterned Japanese kimono with wide sleeves and a wide sash. He holds open scissors, cutting the link on the RIGHT side of a steaming gold diamond. In his other hand he holds up the same kind of tall enamel drip-coffee pot with a long thin spout.

4. to the left of that lower chain. A fourth workman in green turns a key in a small grey metal box. He holds no scissors at all and cuts nothing; a cable runs from his box to a larger machine that sits closed beside the chain.

5. bottom of the picture, below everything. A pale oil slick spreads across the floor. Two workmen tilt one large round flat pan down onto that slick, sliding its rim underneath the oil. Cream-coloured oil pours over the pan's edge.

what must be true when you are done

- every gold diamond has curling wisps rising from it; the plain rings, the rice bowl, the R-flag and the cubes have no wisps at all
- there is exactly one rice bowl and exactly one R-shaped pennant, side by side on the middle chain
- the cola bottle is dark and fizzy and is held up clear of the man's body
- the second and third workmen hold the same kind of drip-coffee pot as each other, and only the third one wears a kimono
- the first workman cuts on the left of his diamond; the third cuts on the right of his diamond; the difference in side must be easy to see
- the man with the key holds no scissors
- three bead-chains, one square scene, not a grid and not split into panels
- warm cream paper background, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind except the one R-shaped flag
