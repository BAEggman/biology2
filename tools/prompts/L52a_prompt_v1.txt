A sunny green hilltop science park, seen from the front. The ground rises gently from left to right. Three areas from left to right: a microscope tower, three spinning swing rides, and a guest cottage with red pleated curtains.

LEFT — THE MICROSCOPE TOWER:
1. A tall white-and-grey tower shaped like a giant old-fashioned light microscope: eyepiece tube on top, objective lens pointing down at a round glass stage. Under the stage a lamp shines yellow light upward as wide, slow, wavy lines.
2. Right beside it stands a slim tall BLACK metal column (an electron microscope). From a round nozzle at its top, a thin straight stream of tiny yellow balls shoots straight down.
3. Two big round screens: the screen on the white tower shows ONE big blurry red blob; the screen on the black column shows TWO sharp, clearly separate red cherries. A small worker holds a huge magnifying glass in front of the blurry screen — the blob looks even bigger but is still one blur.
4. At the foot of the black column: (a) a worker paints a fuzzy round specimen all over with shiny gold paint, and a thin beam sweeps back and forth over its surface in zigzag lines; (b) another worker cuts a big round jelly cell into paper-thin slices with a taut wire, and one thin slice lies on a glowing glass plate under the stream of yellow balls, showing the dark shapes of its insides.

FRONT LEFT — THE SPECIMEN TABLE:
5. A low table with four round glass dishes: a tiny amoeba, a small brown mushroom, a little green frog, a young sprout. On the same table sits a big clear jelly ball: a small cream dome tent in its center, clear jelly around the tent with one small red bean floating in it, and a thin outer skin with tiny doors where white bubbles and grapes go in and dark droplets come out. Next to the jelly ball sits a small pale-yellow pill-shaped bacterium, the same size as the red bean.

MIDDLE — THE SPINNING SWING RIDES:
6. A big glass kitchen blender smashing many little pink round cells into pink soup.
7. Three swing rides in a row, like small carousels with striped canopies, but instead of horses, clear glass tubes hang on chains and fly outward as they spin. The first ride spins slowly with few motion lines, the second faster, the third so fast it is a blur with strong wind lines. Settled at the bottom of the first ride's tubes: tiny cream dome tents. At the bottom of the second ride's tubes: tiny red beans and tiny green pea pods. At the bottom of the third ride's tubes: tiny khaki cloth scraps and tiny purple lumps. Workers carry a tube from one ride to the next and pour its cloudy upper liquid into the next ride's tube, leaving the bottom stuff behind.
8. In the front, a shallow tray of pink dye with round jelly balls cut in half: the big ball has only a thin pink rim and a large white center; the small balls are pink all the way through.

RIGHT — THE GUEST COTTAGE:
9. A big bean-shaped cottage, its front cut away like a dollhouse. Its OUTER wall is smooth cream cloth pierced by a few short open pipes. Just inside is a second wall of RED curtain cloth folded into many deep pleats that reach far into the room. Nearby, a clear jelly-ball cell with little arms and legs dances wildly with sweat drops and is packed full of tiny red beans; another jelly-ball cell sleeps lying down with only one red bean inside.

Beside her stands a big earthen jar of milky rice wine with a gourd dipper.
13. Far right top: a bright sun; an open green pea pod lies on the roof of a small wooden shed; a small blue-green pill-shaped guest climbs into the pod; grapes roll out of the pod down the roof.
