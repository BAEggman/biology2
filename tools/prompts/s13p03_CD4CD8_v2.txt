One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere, and no digits on any badge. / THE SETTING: A pale open plain seen from slightly above. Across the top runs a long grey STONE WALL of blocks, and near its right end the wall is pierced by one round ARCHED HOLE straight through it. / THE WATER: THREE streams and no more - one entering from the left edge, one from the bottom-left corner, one from the bottom-right corner - run inward and meet in a single round POOL in the middle. From that pool ONE stream leaves and pours out through the round hole in the wall. Count them: three in, one out. / THE CROWD: Upper middle, a crowd of about ten townspeople stand shoulder to shoulder. EVERY one of them wears a round rosette BADGE on the chest and EVERY one holds a SMALL ROUND TRAY in both hands. No exceptions. / THE TWO WAISTCOAT MEN - THE BUTTONS ARE THE WHOLE POINT: Two men in open sleeveless waistcoats stand apart from everyone else. Neither wears a badge. Their buttons are drawn LARGE, round, dark, well spaced and clearly separated so they can be counted at a glance. / (A) THE DOUBLE-BREASTED MAN stands at the right edge of the crowd and reaches out one hand to take a SMALL ROUND TRAY from a townsperson. His waistcoat is DOUBLE-BREASTED: it has TWO parallel vertical rows of buttons down the front, a left row and a right row, and each row holds FOUR buttons - four on the left, four on the right, two neat columns of four. / (B) THE SINGLE-BREASTED MAN stands further right and reaches out one hand to take a LARGE RECTANGULAR BOARD. His waistcoat is SINGLE-BREASTED: ONE single vertical row of FOUR buttons down the middle, and no second row anywhere on him. / So one man shows two columns of four and the other shows one column of four. Never swap them, and never give either man a row of any length other than four. / THE BOARD HOLDERS: On the right stand THREE people side by side, each wearing a round rosette badge and each holding a LARGE RECTANGULAR BOARD in both hands - three people, three boards, plainly bigger than the crowd's little trays. A FOURTH person wearing the same badge stands a step apart from those three with BOTH HANDS EMPTY and no board at all. / THE ARCHER AND THE BARE MAN: At the lower left stands a man stripped to the waist with a BARE CHEST and no badge anywhere on him. An archer in a green tunic and cap stands in the lower middle, bow drawn, the arrow pointed straight at that bare-chested man and at nobody else. / MUST NOT APPEAR: writing, letters or numbers of any kind, on badges, trays, boards or anywhere else; a fourth stream; a townsperson without a badge; a waistcoat row with three or five or six buttons. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: exactly three streams meet in one pool and one stream drains through the round hole in the wall. The double-breasted man taking a small round tray has two columns of four buttons; the single-breasted man taking a large board has one column of four buttons. Three badged people each hold one large board and a fourth badged person beside them holds nothing. Every person in the crowd has a badge and a small round tray. One bare-chested man has no badge and the archer aims only at him. One square picture, warm cream paper, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind.
