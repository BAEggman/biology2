Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add ONE new thing. THE ADDITION - a large MEDICINE CAPSULE. Place it lying on the floor at the feet of the HOODED FIGURE on the left, just in front of the hem of its long grey-green cloak, resting flat on the floor and touching nobody. Draw it clearly as a medicine capsule: a smooth rounded cylinder with domed ends, made of two halves that meet in a clean seam across the middle, one half a soft dull red and the other half plain cream, with a faint highlight along the top so it reads as a hard shiny shell. It is closed - the two halves are joined and nothing is inside or spilling out. It is about as long as the hooded figure's forearm, big enough to notice at a glance. Its surface is completely SMOOTH: no spikes, no bristles, no spines of any kind. One capsule only. Nothing else is added. Everything else stays exactly identical: the HOODED FIGURE on the left in its long grey-green cloak with the pointed hood and pale blank mask-face, still holding the rolled paper SCROLL against its body with both hands, and there is still exactly ONE scroll and it stays in its hands; the white sheet-ghost in the middle holding up one small closed loop of ROPE in both hands, exactly one loop; the terracotta POT with the small leafy plant standing on the floor in front of that ghost; the third pale see-through ghost on the right, hollow and empty inside, with its arms held out; the BOY on the right holding the folded orange PAPER BOAT in both hands; the lit MATCH with its flame and the tipping BOTTLE with liquid flying out of it, both in the air above the boy; the plain grey and beige room walls meeting at the corner and the plain floor with its soft shadows; exactly THREE ghost figures and ONE boy in the picture, no more and no fewer; the same warm cream paper, the same thin hand-inked storybook linework and soft flat watercolour washes. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the capsule being drawn as a pill, a tablet, a bean, a stone, a bread roll, an egg, a bottle or a barrel - it must read as a two-piece medicine capsule with a seam across the middle; the capsule having spikes, bristles or spines on it; the capsule being open, broken, or having anything inside it or beside it; a second capsule appearing; the capsule being placed in anyone's hands or under the plant pot; a second scroll, a second rope loop, a second plant or a second paper boat appearing; the hooded figure losing its scroll, its hood or its mask-face; the middle ghost's rope loop being untied or opened; the third ghost stopping being see-through and hollow; the match going out or the bottle stopping pouring; the number of figures changing; any writing, letters or numbers appearing anywhere.
