The attached picture is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not redraw it and do not copy its contents. Study only its look - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, objects on plain cream ground with no background scenery, no text of any kind - and then draw a COMPLETELY NEW picture in exactly that same look. Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered: one workbench, two men, and four objects on the bench. Leave plenty of empty cream paper.

WHAT TO DRAW - one long plain wooden WORKBENCH standing on bare cream ground. TWO workmen in plain tunics and trousers, one at the LEFT end and one at the RIGHT end.

THE LEFT WORKMAN holds a short BRASS SPYGLASS up to one eye; its front lens glows deep RUBY RED. His other hand is pressing a big DARK GREY DROPLET down onto the left end of a long flat wooden BAR lying on the bench.

THE DROPLETS: draw exactly THREE dark grey droplets and no more. Make them BIG - each about the size of the man's fist - and group them in a tight triangle in the air right beside his pressing hand, so they read at a glance as three. Do not scatter small droplets anywhere else in the picture.

THE BAR: a long flat wooden bar, plain and smooth with no markings. On the bench just to the right of it, the same bar has SNAPPED CLEANLY IN HALF into exactly TWO shorter pieces of equal length, lying end to end with a clear gap between them and a few short break-lines in the gap. Exactly two pieces - not three, not four.

THE RIGHT WORKMAN: with his left hand he presses a single flat round brass COIN down into the top face of the LEFT one of those two pieces, so the coin is half sunk into the wood. With his right hand he holds a small ROUND wooden SHIELD - a plain circular disc with a rim and a boss in the middle - facing the RIGHT one of the two pieces, and that piece has three or four short sparkle lines around it as if freshly polished bright.

Nothing else is on the bench or in the picture. No tools, no boxes, no plants, no other objects.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: more or fewer than three droplets anywhere; the droplets being drawn small or scattered instead of three big ones in one tight group; the snapped bar being in three or more pieces, or not snapped at all; extra bars, sticks or planks appearing on the bench; the bar being drawn with segments, squares, notches, beads or divisions - it is plain smooth wood; any drawing pins, nails, screws or studs appearing in the bar; more than one coin appearing; the coin being drawn as a medal, button, gear or clock; the shield being drawn as a pointed heraldic shield, a kite shield, a mirror, a plate, a drum, a lid or a tray - it must be a plain ROUND circular wooden shield; a third person appearing; the spyglass lens being any colour but deep ruby red; the spyglass being drawn as a telescope on a tripod, a microscope, a lamp or a torch; a second spyglass appearing; any plant, leaf, sun, sunbeam, cloud, building, room, wall, floor or background scenery; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
