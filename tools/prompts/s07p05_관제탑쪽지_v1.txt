[시상하부 방출호르몬 셋 — 관제탑이 파이프로 내려보낸다 · 첫 프롬프트]

Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Tall image, clearly taller than it is wide. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - a small square CONTROL TOWER with a flat roof, standing at the TOP of the picture. It has one wide window on its front, and the window stands OPEN. Hinged above that window is a flat wooden SHUTTER, currently swung up and out of the way.

THE BIG PIPE: from the window sill, one thick metal PIPE runs straight DOWN the middle of the picture to the bottom edge. The front of this pipe is CUT AWAY along its whole length so the viewer sees straight into it, like a pipe sliced open lengthwise.

WHAT IS FALLING INSIDE THE PIPE: THREE small PARACHUTES are drifting down inside the cut-open pipe, well spaced apart one above the other, all three of the same shape and size. A different object hangs from each parachute:
  - from the TOP parachute hangs one shallow DISH shaped like a butterfly with two rounded wings joined at the middle;
  - from the MIDDLE parachute hangs one small round LAYER CAKE with three visible tiers stacked up;
  - from the BOTTOM parachute hangs a pair of plain EGGS, exactly two, tied together side by side.
Each object is drawn plainly and is easy to tell apart from the other two.

THE RETURN LINE: a much THINNER pipe runs UP the picture alongside the big one, from the bottom edge back to the tower. Its top end reaches the hinged shutter and PUSHES it, so the shutter is just starting to swing down toward the open window - the window is not shut yet, only beginning to close.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no animals, no buildings other than the tower, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of parachutes other than three; two parachutes carrying the same object; the three parachutes drawn outside the pipe instead of inside it; the big pipe drawn closed so its inside cannot be seen; the butterfly dish drawn as an actual live butterfly with legs or antennae - it is a shallow dish with a butterfly outline; the cake drawn with any number of tiers other than three; any number of eggs other than two; the thin return pipe pushing anywhere other than the shutter; the shutter drawn fully closed over the window, or lying flat and untouched - it must be caught midway, plainly being pushed down; the tower standing at the bottom instead of the top; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
