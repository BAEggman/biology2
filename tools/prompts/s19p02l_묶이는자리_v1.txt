Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered. This is a CLOSE-UP: the objects fill the frame.

WHAT TO DRAW - TWO long bundles of dry straw lying HORIZONTALLY across the picture, one behind the other, seen from the side. Each bundle is thick along its whole length EXCEPT at ONE single place near the middle of the picture, where BOTH bundles pinch in and become narrow, and their loose straws twist together into ONE narrow twisted WAIST that joins the two bundles. There is only ONE such waist in the whole picture; everywhere else the two bundles are thick and clearly separate from each other.

ON TOP OF THE WAIST: two small flat METAL PLATES are bolted onto the upper surface of the waist, side by side and BACK TO BACK, one belonging to the left bundle and one to the right bundle. Each plate has visible bolt heads. On each plate there is exactly ONE small iron HOOK. The hook on the LEFT plate curves only to the LEFT. The hook on the RIGHT plate curves only to the RIGHT. The two hooks point away from each other.

THE TWO ROPES: one thick taut ROPE comes in from beyond the LEFT edge of the picture and is caught on the LEFT hook. A second thick taut rope comes in from beyond the RIGHT edge and is caught on the RIGHT hook. Both ropes are pulled straight and tight. The ropes touch NOTHING except their own hook - they do not wrap around the straw anywhere.

INSIDE THE WAIST: the twisted straw of the waist passes through FIVE small wooden SPOOLS threaded on it like beads in a single row, so the straw winds around each spool. FOUR of the spools are plain pale wood. The MIDDLE spool - the third of the five - is DARK NAVY BLUE, plainly a different colour from the other four. Standing upright on top of that dark navy spool is one small wooden STEPLADDER, opened into an A shape.

UNDER THE WAIST: one single METAL RING clasps both bundles together from below, directly beneath the waist. This is the only ring in the picture - there is no ring, band, chain or tie anywhere else along either bundle.

SITTING ON THE RING: one small carved STONE GUARDIAN FIGURE, like a little roadside stone statue with a wide flat hat, sits on top of that ring and spreads over it so that the ring is sheltered underneath the statue.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no faces, no tools, no buildings, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: more than one narrow waist, or the two bundles pinching together at several places; the bundles being drawn thin all along so the waist cannot be seen; more or fewer than two metal plates; the two hooks curving the same way, or both curving inward toward each other - they must point in OPPOSITE directions; a rope wrapped around the straw bundles instead of caught on a hook; any number of spools other than five; the dark navy spool being anywhere other than the exact middle of the five; more than one stepladder, or the stepladder standing on a pale wooden spool instead of the dark navy one; more than one metal ring, or extra bands and chains drawn along the bundles; the stone statue being drawn as a person, an animal or a bird instead of a small carved stone figure; the statue sitting anywhere other than on the ring; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
