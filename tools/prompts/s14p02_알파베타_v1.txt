One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. The ONLY marks that look like letters anywhere are the two Greek letters carved on the bricks described in part 1; nothing else carries writing or numbers. / THE SETTING: A cutaway view into the inside of a cell drawn as a builder's yard, with several long structures lying across it and two round discs standing at the right. / 1. THE THICK HOLLOW PIPE - AND THE ONE NEW THING: Running across the upper part of the picture lies the THICKEST structure of all, a hollow round PIPE wide enough for a person to crawl into; its open mouth faces the viewer so the hollow inside is plainly visible. Its wall is built of BRICKS laid in a spiral course, and the bricks ALTERNATE between two kinds all the way along: one brick carries a carved Greek letter ALPHA and the next carries a carved Greek letter BETA, then alpha, then beta, over and over, never two of the same kind side by side. Carve the letters LARGE and deep so the alternation is obvious at a glance. / 2. THE THINNEST CORD: Below it lies the THINNEST structure, a slender pale apricot-coloured cord of two strands twisted around each other. It is plainly the thinnest thing in the picture. / 3. THE MIDDLE ROPE: Between them lies a rope of MIDDLE thickness, plainly thicker than the apricot cord and plainly thinner than the brick pipe, plaited from many fibres. Dust and a cobweb hang on it and nobody is on it. / 4. THE CLIMBERS: TWO climbers are on the thick brick pipe: one walks along it head-first toward one end, the other walks head-first toward the opposite end. ONE more climber walks along the thin apricot cord. Nobody at all is on the middle rope. / 5. THE TWO DISCS, RIGHT: Two round discs stand on edge like wheels. The UPPER disc is EMPTY in the centre and its rim is made of NINE bundles evenly spaced around it, each bundle a group of THREE rods. The LOWER disc has NINE bundles of TWO rods around its rim and, in addition, TWO single rods standing in its centre. / WHAT MUST NOT BE IN THE PICTURE: any writing other than the carved alpha and beta; numbers; a climber on the middle rope. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: the brick pipe is the thickest and its wall alternates carved alpha and beta bricks; the apricot twisted cord is the thinnest; the plaited rope is in between and carries dust, a cobweb and nobody; two climbers on the pipe going opposite ways and one on the thin cord; one disc with nine groups of three rods and an empty centre, one disc with nine groups of two rods and two rods in the centre. One square picture, warm cream paper, thin hand-drawn linework.
