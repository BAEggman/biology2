Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - TWO round TENTS standing side by side on bare ground, well apart, each cut away down the front so the viewer sees inside. Both tents are the same size and shape. Each tent wall has THREE round holes through it, and set into each hole is a small metal TURNSTILE with three horizontal arms.

INSIDE EACH TENT: one loose tangle of THIN THREAD lies on the floor, and one smooth round BALL sits beside it.

THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE TWO TENTS is the size of that ball and how busy the ground outside is:
  - the LEFT tent's ball is SMALL, about the size of a plum, and on the bare ground outside that tent there are only THREE small paired objects.
  - the RIGHT tent's ball is BIG, three or four times as wide as the left one, filling much of the tent, and on the ground outside that tent there are MANY of the same paired objects, a dozen or so, scattered about.

WHAT THE PAIRED OBJECTS LOOK LIKE: each one is a LARGE rounded lump with a SMALLER rounded lump sitting snugly on top of it, like a big bun with a little bun nested into it - two pieces plainly locked together as one.

AT THE RIGHT TENT, show where they come from: from the big ball inside, ONE LARGE lump and ONE SMALL lump have come loose and are rolling away separately, and they leave through TWO DIFFERENT turnstiles - the large one squeezing through one turnstile and the small one through another turnstile some distance away. Just outside the tent, past the turnstiles, that large lump and that small lump are shown meeting and locking together into one of the paired objects.

ONE of the paired objects lying outside the right tent has a string of small round BEADS running out of it, trailing away across the ground.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no animals, no furniture, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the two tents being drawn different sizes or shapes - only the BALL inside differs; any number of turnstiles per tent other than three; the left tent's ball drawn as large as the right tent's; the left tent having many paired objects outside, or the right tent having only a few; a paired object drawn as a single lump instead of two locked pieces; the large and small lump leaving through the SAME turnstile, or leaving already joined together - they must travel separately and meet only outside; the beads coming out of the tent or out of a lone lump instead of out of a joined pair; a thread tangle missing from either tent; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
