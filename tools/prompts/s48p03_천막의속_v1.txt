Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens and a little brick red, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered. This is a CLOSE-UP: the objects fill the frame.

WHAT TO DRAW - one round TENT standing on bare ground, cut away down the front so the viewer can see straight into its inside.

THE TENT FABRIC IS TWO SEPARATE LAYERS: an outer sheet and an inner sheet with a narrow gap of air between them, plainly drawn as two lines with a space, like a double-walled tent. Make this double layer obvious wherever the cut edge is visible.

THE HOLES: FOUR round HOLES pass straight through BOTH layers at once, spaced apart around the tent wall. Set into each hole is a small metal TURNSTILE, the kind with three horizontal arms that a person pushes through one at a time. One small person is halfway through one of the turnstiles, pushing an arm around; the other three turnstiles are empty.

THE NET INSIDE: the whole INNER surface of the tent is lined with a NET that braces it from within. The net is made of long WAVY CRINKLED NOODLE STRANDS - unmistakably instant-noodle strands, curly and wiggly - and several strands are twisted together into medium-thick cords, and those cords are woven into a mesh. The noodle texture must be obvious.

THE BROKEN PLACES: on THREE spots of that noodle net there is a round BRICK-RED INK STAMP MARK, printed flat on the cords like a seal. At every one of those three stamped spots the cord is SNAPPED, its two cut ends curling apart. Where there is no stamp the net is whole and taut.

THE SHED: on the right, a low flat-roofed SHED stands against the tent, and the tent's outer fabric runs on into the shed's roof WITHOUT ANY BREAK, seam or edge - one continuous sheet, so there is no telling where the tent stops and the shed starts.

Nothing else is in the picture. No other people, no animals, no tools, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the tent fabric drawn as a single layer instead of two separate sheets with a gap; any number of holes other than four; a hole that pierces only one of the two layers; a hole without a turnstile in it; more than one person, or the person standing beside a turnstile instead of halfway through one; the inner net drawn as plain rope, wire or string instead of clearly wavy crinkled noodle strands; the noodles drawn as loose separate strands instead of twisted into cords and woven into a mesh; any number of stamp marks other than three; a stamp mark sitting on an unbroken cord, or a snapped cord with no stamp on it - stamp and break always go together; a visible seam, join or edge between the tent fabric and the shed roof; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
