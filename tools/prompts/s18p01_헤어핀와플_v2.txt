Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add TWO new things - one on the flat rock in the lower middle and one on the shelf. Nothing else is added anywhere. THE FIRST ADDITION - a large metal HAIRPIN. Lay it flat on the same broad flat rock ledge that the brass trombone already lies on, in the empty space just to the LEFT of the trombone's flared bell, directly below the bald man in the WHITE COAT. It lies alone on that rock, NOT touching the trombone and not touching anybody. Draw it clearly as a hairpin seen from the side: a single length of dark springy wire bent double into a long narrow U, so that it has one rounded closed end and two prongs that lie close together and end in small blunt tips; one prong is straight and the other has a few shallow waves along it, the way a real hairpin does. It is plain dark metal, about half as long as the trombone. It is ONE piece only, closed at its rounded end. THE SECOND ADDITION - a single WAFFLE. Stand it upright on the TOP of the tall WOODEN SHELF at the upper right, in the empty space to the RIGHT of the two grey helmets that already sit up there, leaning against the shelf and touching nothing else. Draw it as one square golden-brown waffle with a deep grid of square hollows pressed right across its face, its edges slightly crisp and uneven. It is plain - no syrup, no butter, no plate, no fruit, nothing on it and nothing under it. One waffle only, and it is the only food in the picture. Everything else stays exactly identical, and these things matter most - do not add, remove, move, cover or re-count a single one of them: THE PALE FROZEN JELLY SLAB AT THE LOWER LEFT - the soft blue-white slab that lies wedged over the rock at the far lower left and spills down over the edge of the crevasse below it - MUST STAY EXACTLY WHERE IT IS, the same size, the same shape and the same colour.
