One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE UPPER THIRD: One tree stands at the top of the square, its trunk cut open down the middle so you can see inside. Two men stand high on the trunk, leaning back and hauling hard on one taut rope that runs straight down the inside of the trunk. Their feet are braced and the rope is stretched tight. / THE LOWER TWO THIRDS, SEEN FROM ABOVE: A big round estate drawn as a flat circle seen from directly overhead, filling the bottom of the square. Small square stone APARTMENT BLOCKS are set in three concentric rings around a central courtyard. In that courtyard stands a tight bundle of upright PIPES in pale colours - blue, green and rust. Narrow open ALLEYS run between the blocks, all the way round. Every block has one plain door on its outer face and one plain door on its inner face, and those doors are completely plain and undecorated - flat panels, no carving, no window, no handle plate, no ornament of any kind. Small footbridges join neighbouring blocks to each other. / THE TWO LINES OF PEOPLE: One line of small people walks along the ALLEYS ONLY, weaving between the blocks and never entering any door. A second line of small people walks STRAIGHT THROUGH the blocks - in one plain door and out the other, straight on to the next block. / THE ONE NEW THING - THE BAND: One continuous BLACK PIPE runs all the way round the estate in a complete ring, one ring in from the outside, lying across the alleys. It is a heavy black industrial pipe with round flanged joints, the kind that carries gas. Wrapped around that black pipe along its whole length are pale tan CORK BOARDS - flat speckled cork planks bound on with dark metal straps, so the ring reads as a black pipe jacketed in cork. The ring sits in the alleys and blocks them. It does NOT cross any doorway: every plain door stands clear of it. / One walker in an alley has come up against the cork-wrapped ring, stopped, and is turning aside into the nearest plain door. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: The blocks are plainly APARTMENT blocks in concentric rings around one courtyard of upright pipes. Every door is flat and completely plain, with no ornament. One line of people uses only the alleys; another line goes through the doors. The black ring is a pipe with flanged joints, wrapped in pale speckled cork boards held by metal straps, and it lies across the alleys only, never across a door.
