★ 시계로 그리지 않는다 — 회중시계(생체시계)·시계탑(광주기)이 이미 예약이다. 위에서 내려다본 둥근 방으로 그린다.

Draw another picture in exactly the same style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - a big round room seen from directly above, like a pie seen from the top, divided by low straight walls into exactly FOUR wedge-shaped rooms. The four wedges are plainly DIFFERENT SIZES. Going clockwise from the top:

WEDGE 1 is by far the BIGGEST - it takes up about half of the whole circle. Inside it sits one round lump of risen bread dough, domed and swollen.

WEDGE 2 is medium. Inside it lies one iron bar bent into the shape of a letter S, and beside it TWO identical loaves of that same dough, side by side.

WEDGE 3 is medium. Inside it the same two loaves lie side by side with one large round MAGNIFYING GLASS resting flat on top of them, its handle sticking out.

WEDGE 4 is by far the SMALLEST - a narrow slice. Inside it the two loaves have been dragged apart to opposite corners of the wedge, with bare floor between them.

In the OUTER wall of wedge 1 there is a low doorway, and through it, OUTSIDE the round room entirely, is a small separate square room. In that small room, lying still on the floor, are two things and nothing else: a dry piece of wood with many bare branches spreading from it, and a thick bundle with straight stripes banding across it.

At the BOTTOM RIGHT of the picture, well clear of the big room, is a SECOND round room, much smaller, seen from above the same way, divided into only TWO wedges: one wedge holds an iron bar bent into a letter S, the other holds two loaves dragged apart. Beside this small round room, laid out in a row on the bare ground, are FIVE round balls, each one smaller than the one before it, from biggest on the left to smallest on the right.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no hands, no clock faces, no clock hands, no numbers on any dial.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the big room divided into any number of wedges other than four; the four wedges drawn the same size - wedge 1 must be about half the circle and wedge 4 must be the narrowest slice, and this must be obvious at a glance; wedge 1 given two loaves instead of one lump; wedge 2 or wedge 3 given one loaf instead of two; the S-shaped bar drawn as a snake, a rope or a worm - it is a stiff iron bar bent into an S; the magnifying glass appearing in any wedge but wedge 3; the two loaves in wedge 4 drawn touching each other; the small square side room drawn INSIDE the circle instead of outside it; the branched wood or the striped bundle appearing anywhere except in that small side room; the second round room divided into more than two wedges; any number of balls other than five, or the balls drawn all the same size; anything drawn to look like a clock face, clock hand or dial; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere - the S-shaped iron bars are the only letter shapes allowed.
