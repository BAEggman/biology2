Draw another picture in exactly the same style as the one you just made - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - at the upper left a glass PRISM, a single white beam entering it, and out of it a RAINBOW BAND fanning downward and to the right, landing across a long horizontal BENCH that runs the full width of the lower half of the picture. The colours of the band land on the bench in this order: violet and blue at the LEFT end of the bench, GREEN clearly in the MIDDLE, orange and red at the RIGHT end.

Lying along the top of the bench, running its whole length through every colour of the band, is one long thin GREEN THREAD, like a single strand of green yarn.

Crowded onto that thread are many tiny tadpole shaped swimmers. They gather in a DENSE CROWD where the BLUE part of the band falls on the thread, and in a second DENSE CROWD where the RED part of the band falls, and there are NONE AT ALL on the stretch of thread where the GREEN part of the band falls - that middle stretch is completely bare.

Rising from the thread at each of the two crowded places are a few small PALE BUBBLES drifting upward.

On the wall above the bench hang exactly TWO FRAMED PICTURES side by side, the same size as each other. Inside the LEFT frame is a paint pot tipped up, drinking a beam of light. Inside the RIGHT frame is a small factory building puffing smoke out of its chimney. Place the bench so that it sits directly beneath the RIGHT frame.

Nothing else is in the picture. No people, no sun, no other furniture.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the rainbow band drawn with green at an end instead of in the middle; the thread drawn broken, or drawn short so it does not reach through every colour; swimmers appearing on the green stretch - that stretch must be completely bare; the two crowds not being obviously denser than the rest of the thread; any number of frames other than two, or the two frames holding the same picture; the paint pot and the factory swapped between frames; the bench placed under the LEFT frame; bubbles rising from the green stretch; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
