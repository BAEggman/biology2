the layout

One tall teal-green post stands straight down the middle of the square, dividing it into a LEFT half and a RIGHT half. A thin horizontal ink line divides the LEFT half again into an upper and a lower scene. The RIGHT half is one single scene.

left half, upper scene

A bearded man in a teal jumper and mustard trousers faces right, pushing one round-ringed key into a keyhole plate. That plate is bolted ONTO a heavy iron-barred gate — plate and gate are one piece of ironwork. The gate has swung open and a stream of small grey beads pours out through the opening. Rising up just past the open gate is one large bare human arm bent at the elbow, fist up, the biceps bulging.

left half, lower scene

The same man, the same iron gate with its bolted-on plate, the same open gate, the same grey beads. But here NO arm and no muscle of any kind. Instead, beyond the gate a SECOND man in a grey shirt pushes his own round-ringed key into a second plate on a second iron gate — one man passing the same act to the next.

right half

Flat on the bare wall hangs one small keyhole plate on its own — no gate, no door, no bars, no hinges, nothing attached behind it.

★ A THIRD bearded man in a teal jumper and mustard trousers stands at that plate and pushes one round-ringed key into its keyhole — the same key, grip and pose as the two men on the left. His hand and the key are clearly in the keyhole. This plate is NOT bare and NOT unattended.

From that plate a wide pale corridor runs off to the right and splits into a clear Y: it runs along, then divides into two branches, one climbing to the upper right and one dropping to the lower right, a plain wedge of wall between them. Along it runs one man in a red vest carrying one round flat disc.

At the end of the upper branch, a small room: a pendulum hangs on a cord from its ceiling and a man in blue-grey overalls has caught the round bob in his hand and holds it still.

At the end of the lower branch is a second small room, where one hand grips the narrow neck of a soft water bag and squeezes it so three drops fall from its bottom.
