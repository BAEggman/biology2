Work only from the attached picture. Redraw the attached picture keeping everything exactly as it is, and add ONE new thing. THE ADDITION - a single cut DIAMOND, falling through the air. Put it in the empty air just below the hands of the UPSIDE-DOWN CLIMBER - the man on the thick grey pillar whose head points downwards - so that it looks as if it has just slipped from him and is dropping straight down. It hangs in mid-air, well ABOVE the floor and touching nobody and nothing. Draw it as one clear gem cut with flat angular facets: a broad flat table on top, sloping facets around it and a point at the bottom, drawn with thin ink lines and a pale blue-white wash, with two or three tiny sparkle lines beside it to show it is falling. It is about the size of the man's head. One diamond only, and it is the only gem in the picture. Nothing else is added. Everything else stays exactly identical, and the counted things matter most - do not add, remove, move or re-count a single one of them: at the foot of the thick grey pillar there are still exactly TWO things on the floor and no more - ONE smooth white EGG on its left and ONE neatly FOLDED WHITE CLOTH on its right - and NOTHING new may be put on the floor beside them; the foot of the thin apricot cord stays completely EMPTY, with nothing on the floor there; the three uprights keep their exact thicknesses and order - the THICK hollow grey pillar on the left, the MIDDLE-thick twisted rope in the centre, and the THINNEST apricot twisted cord on the right; exactly THREE climbers and no more - TWO on the thick grey pillar (one climbing head-up, one hanging head-down) and ONE on the thinnest apricot cord; the middle twisted rope still has NOBODY on it and still carries its grey dust and cobwebs; the wooden roof beams, the plain wooden floorboards, the window at the left with its glazing bars, and the small table below it with the few books stacked on it; the same warm cream paper, the same thin hand-inked storybook linework and soft flat watercolour washes. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the diamond touching the floor, being placed beside the white egg or the folded cloth, or landing at the foot of any of the three uprights - it must stay in the air; the diamond being drawn as a round jewel, a bead, a pebble, a marble, a drop of water, an ice cube, a star or a ring - it is a cut gem with flat angular facets and a point at the bottom; the diamond being coloured red, green or yellow - it is clear and pale; a second diamond or any other gem appearing; anyone holding or catching the diamond; a third thing appearing on the floor at the foot of the thick pillar, or anything at all appearing on the floor at the foot of the thin cord or the middle rope; the number of climbers changing from three, or any climber moving to a different upright; the upside-down climber turning the right way up, or the head-up climber turning over; the middle rope gaining a climber or losing its dust and cobwebs; the three thicknesses changing or swapping places; the window, the table or the books disappearing; any writing, letters or numbers appearing anywhere.
