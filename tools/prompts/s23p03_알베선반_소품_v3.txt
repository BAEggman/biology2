Draw four small separate objects on a plain, flat, light cream background, arranged in a 2-by-2 grid with a lot of empty space between them. Each object is a small wooden wall shelf — a short plank resting on two small dark iron brackets, seen from the front and slightly from above. Top-left: the shelf holds ONE white egg lying on it. Bottom-left: the shelf holds TWO white eggs lying side by side. Top-right: the shelf holds ONE small, neatly folded white cloth, like a folded linen napkin. Bottom-right: the shelf holds TWO small, neatly folded white cloths side by side. All four shelves are the same size. Style: hand-drawn coloured-pencil illustration with thin dark ink outlines and soft pencil shading, warm muted palette, like a children's storybook. No wall, no floor, no cast shadows on the background, no people, no writing, no labels, no numbers.
