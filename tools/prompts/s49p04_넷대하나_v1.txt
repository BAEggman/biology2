Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about twice as wide as it is tall. Slightly raised three-quarter view. Keep it readable.

WHAT TO DRAW - a large upright GLASS TANK standing a little right of centre, with clear water and small round GRAPES floating in it, and one broad GREEN BAND painted horizontally around its middle.

ON THE LEFT, FOUR wooden CHUTES run down side by side and all four empty into the top of that tank. Grapes are tumbling down every one of the four. Beside each chute is a big black ARROW pointing UP - four arrows in all. At the upper end of each chute stands one thing, and the four are different:

  FIRST CHUTE (nearest the top left): a man swinging a heavy wooden CLUB at a bulging sack of grapes.
  SECOND CHUTE: a person hugging an enormous CORK STOPPER, as tall as they are, shaped like a wine cork. Right beside them stands a big STRONGMAN whose bare arms have gone thin and slack, and a bulging SACK is sliding off that strongman's shoulder into this chute.
  THIRD CHUTE: a runner in athletic clothes holding out a short plain BATON.
  FOURTH CHUTE: a metal DRUM whose lid has burst off and is flying away, with grapes bursting up out of the open drum.

ON THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE TANK stands exactly ONE person, alone, in long WHITE CLOTHES, holding a SYRINGE raised high in one hand and with the other working a long pole that lifts grapes OUT of the tank and tips them away to the right. Beside that person is ONE big black ARROW pointing DOWN. There is no chute on this side and nobody else stands with them.

The picture must read at a glance as FOUR on the left against ONE on the right.

Nothing else is in the picture. No buildings, no trees, no animals, no crowd.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of chutes other than four, or any number of up-arrows other than four; more than one person on the right, or any chute on the right; the strongman drawn with big muscular arms - his arms must look thin and emptied; the cork stopper drawn as a bottle, barrel, cylinder of stone or a plain post - it must read as a wine cork, wider at the top and slightly tapered; the baton drawn as a club, torch, hammer or rolled paper - it is a plain smooth rod; the drum drawn with its lid still on; grapes flowing the wrong way in any chute - all four must pour INTO the tank; the person in white pouring grapes in instead of lifting them out; any writing, letters, numbers or labels appearing anywhere.
