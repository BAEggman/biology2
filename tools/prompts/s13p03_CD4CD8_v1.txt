No exceptions in this crowd. / THE FOUR APART: On the right stand exactly FOUR people, well away from the crowd. Each of them also wears the same round chest badge, but only THREE of the four hold a LARGE RECTANGULAR BOARD - big flat boards, plainly bigger than the crowd's little trays. The fourth of them holds nothing at all and stands empty-handed. / THE TWO NEW FIGURES - COUNT THE BUTTONS, THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART: Two extra men are added, each wearing an open sleeveless waistcoat that buttons up the front, and on each waistcoat the buttons are drawn LARGE, round, dark, evenly spaced and clearly separated so anyone can count them at a glance. / (A) Standing at the edge of the CROWD, reaching out one hand to take a SMALL ROUND TRAY from a townsperson, is a man whose waistcoat has EXACTLY EIGHT buttons in one straight vertical row down the front - eight, no more and no fewer. / (B) Standing beside the FOUR APART, reaching out one hand to take a LARGE RECTANGULAR BOARD, is a man whose waistcoat has EXACTLY FOUR buttons in one straight vertical row down the front - four, no more and no fewer. / Neither of these two men wears a chest badge; their waistcoats are open and plain so the buttons are the only thing on them.
