the setting

A stone-walled canal of pale green water runs diagonally across the square from the lower left to the upper right. Flat stone quays run along both banks. Plain stone buildings with a mustard-yellow roof stand on the far bank at the right.

lower left — the gate

A man in a long brown coat holds one big iron key and has just swung open a wooden gate. On that gate hangs one black iron padlock, plainly open, its shackle swung clear. He has walked in from off the picture — he does not belong to the yard.

Through the open gate walks a line of porters, each with a plump WHITE sack shouldered. One of them is tipping his sack over the water so a stream of white powder pours into the canal. A neat stack of spare white sacks sits on the quay behind them.

★ the one new thing

Right beside that line of porters, on the open stone of the quay, a scatter of white grains has spilled onto the ground. One porter has knelt down and is sweeping those spilled grains with a flat metal HAND SCOOP back into a round wooden TUB standing beside him. He is plainly gathering them up and putting them back — the tub is half full of the same white grains, and his scoop is mid-stroke. He uses a hand scoop, not a broom; there is no broom anywhere in the picture.

upper left — across the water

On the far quay at the top left stands one man in a crested Roman soldier's helmet, a short sword in one hand. With the other hand he tips a small metal pail so that small pale grains spill out of it and fall into the canal.

the middle of the near quay

Standing on the near quay, one man tips a wooden crate and a heap of bright YELLOW LEMONS tumbles out onto the stone. Just beside the lemons lies one CRACKED EGG, its two shell halves apart and the yellow yolk sitting on the stone.

A little further right, a second man tips a metal pail of WHITE CHALK POWDER out onto the quay. Lying flat on the stone beside him is one rolled bolt of plain WHITE CLOTH.

upper right — the roof

On the mustard-yellow roof, two men stand facing each other and pass between them one white V-SHAPED pipe coupling, holding it up as though about to fit it into the run of white pipes that goes along the roof edge and down the wall.

lower middle — the water

Crouching at the water's edge, one man in a dark blue uniform and a peaked policeman's cap holds a white hose in one hand and raises the other hand to his mouth, tasting the water.
