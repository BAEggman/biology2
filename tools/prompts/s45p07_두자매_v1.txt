Draw another picture in exactly the same style as the one you just made - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Square image. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - two standing figures side by side, facing the viewer, built the same way as each other. Each figure has, in place of a head, a flat SQUARE FRAME washed PURPLE-VIOLET, held up on a short neck; at the exact centre of each square frame sits one small SILVER BALL. From the bottom of each figure hangs one single LONG STRAIGHT TAIL that drops down and plunges into a STRIPED MAT lying flat across the bottom of the picture, so that the end of each tail disappears inside the mat.

The two figures are identical in every way except ONE: hanging at the LEFT figure's neck is a small THREE PRONGED TWIG, like a tiny bare branch with three tips. Hanging at the RIGHT figure's neck is a small FORK. Nothing else differs between them.

Out of the LEFT figure's square frame shines a beam of clear BLUE light. Out of the RIGHT figure's square frame shines a beam of slightly GREENISH BLUE light. The two beams are close in colour but not the same.

Far back at the right edge, behind and beyond the two figures, stand TWO faint distant SILHOUETTES of the very same shape, small and pale, standing under a DEEP RED glow.

Nothing else is in the picture. No furniture, no plants, no sun.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: the two square frames drawn any colour other than purple-violet; the silver ball missing from either frame, or drawn off centre; either figure drawn with more than one tail, or with a tail that stops short of the mat instead of plunging into it; the mat drawn plain instead of striped; the twig and the fork swapped, or both figures given the same neck piece, or either figure given both; the twig drawn with any number of prongs other than three; the two beams drawn in obviously different colours such as blue and red - they must be near neighbours; the distant silhouettes drawn the same size as the two figures instead of small and faint; the red glow placed over the two main figures instead of only over the distant pair; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
