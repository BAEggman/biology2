One wide horizontal illustration, about twice as wide as it is tall, in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE SETTING: THREE market shopfronts stand side by side in one row, seen straight on from the front. All three must be COMPLETE and fully inside the picture with clear cream space at both ends - none of them may be cut off by the edge of the frame. They are the same size and the same shape as one another; only the details listed below differ. Each shop has a striped awning, an open serving counter, a shelf on the back wall, a line of sausages hung across the top, a weighing scale on the counter and one shopkeeper standing behind it. / 1. THE AWNING STRIPES: The LEFT shop's awning has plain straight stripes running straight down. The RIGHT shop's awning has plain straight stripes running straight down, exactly like the left one. The MIDDLE shop's awning is different from both: every one of its stripes FORKS partway down into two or three branches, like bare tree twigs, so the middle awning alone looks branched. This is the only shop with branched stripes. / 2. THE INSIDE OF EACH SHOP: The LEFT and MIDDLE shops are each one single open room behind the counter with no partitions at all. The RIGHT shop alone is divided inside into several small compartments by thin partition walls, and the compartment in the MIDDLE of that shop is noticeably LARGER than the others. / 3. THE SCALES: The LEFT and MIDDLE shops each have the same SMALL kitchen scale with a little dial. The RIGHT shop alone has a much BIGGER scale, a tall platform scale with a large round dial face, plainly larger than the other two. / 4. THE THREAD: On the back shelf of the MIDDLE shop and of the RIGHT shop stands a wooden SPOOL with thread neatly wound on it. On the back shelf of the LEFT shop there is no spool at all - only a loose messy TANGLE of thread lying in a heap. / 5. THE SAUSAGES: In the LEFT shop and the MIDDLE shop the sausages hang as one continuous STRING, all threaded together on a single line. In the RIGHT shop the sausages hang SEPARATELY, each one on its own short hook with a gap between them, never threaded together. / 6. THE SHOPKEEPERS - AND THE ONE NEW THING: Each shop has one shopkeeper standing behind the counter. The MIDDLE and RIGHT shopkeepers stand empty-handed with nothing in their hands. The LEFT shopkeeper alone holds up a long slim wooden POLE whose top end is bent into the shape of a capital letter F - one upright with two short arms sticking out to the same side, one at the very top and one halfway down, like a flag bracket. He holds it upright and clear of his body so its F shape reads at once. No other shopkeeper has a pole of any kind. / WHAT MUST NOT BE IN THE PICTURE: a hat or cap on any shopkeeper's head; a fourth shop; any shop cut off by the frame; writing, letters or numbers anywhere - the pole's F shape is a bent piece of wood, not a written letter. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: three complete shops in a row with space at both ends. Only the middle awning has branched stripes. Only the right shop is divided into compartments with a bigger middle one, and only it has the big platform scale and separately hung sausages. Only the left shop has a loose tangle of thread instead of a spool. Only the left shopkeeper holds an F-shaped wooden pole, and nobody wears a hat. One wide picture, warm cream paper, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind.
