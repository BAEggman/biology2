THE BUTTER BAND: across the middle of the picture, from the left edge to the right edge, runs one flat horizontal band of translucent yellow BUTTER — a cut slice seen exactly from the front (we do NOT see its top surface). Its thickness is about one quarter of the picture height. Because the butter is translucent, we can see the legs inside it.

THE BUOY PEOPLE: funny little creatures that are only a round fishing-buoy head with a white stripe and two long thin legs — no body, no arms. They live in two rows along the band:
- TOP ROW: each RED head sits right on the top edge of the band, in the water above; its two legs go DOWN INSIDE the butter to the middle of the band.
- BOTTOM ROW (a mirror image): each BLUE head hangs right under the bottom edge of the band, in the water below; its two legs go UP INSIDE the butter to the middle of the band.
So every head is outside the band, in water, and every leg is inside the butter. No head is ever inside the butter and nobody stands in front of the band.
