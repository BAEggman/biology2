Clean cartoon illustration in the style of a medical mnemonic scene: bold black ink outlines, flat bright colors, big expressive characters, simple uncluttered pale background, no text, letters or numbers anywhere. A single wide 16:9 picture.

CENTER: a small two-storey house standing on short stilts, drawn as a CUTAWAY (the front wall removed) so we see FOUR rooms inside: two SMALL rooms upstairs and two BIG rooms downstairs. A thick solid wall runs down the middle of the house from roof to floor with NO door or opening in it at all. The two rooms on the LEFT half are filled with grey-blue water; the two rooms on the RIGHT half are filled with bright red water. The walls of the BOTTOM-RIGHT room are about THREE TIMES thicker than every other wall, so that room looks narrower inside.
- In the floor between each upstairs room and the room below it there is a hinged TRAPDOOR hanging open downward: the left trapdoor is made of exactly THREE flaps, the right trapdoor of exactly TWO flaps.
- Each downstairs room has an exit door in its OUTER side wall (left room: in the left wall; right room: in the right wall). Each exit door has a CRESCENT-MOON shaped cut-out window (like an old outhouse door), swings OUTWARD only, and has a wooden prop wedged behind it so it cannot swing back.

ON THE ROOF: two big pink spongy LUNG balloons. Little white round bubbles float into them, and little dark grey bubbles with smoke tails float out of them.

PIPES (grey-blue pipes = blood with little oxygen; bright red pipes = blood full of oxygen; small white arrows on every pipe show the flow):
- A GREY-BLUE pipe leaves the exit door of the bottom-LEFT room, climbs up the outside of the house to the lung balloons.
- A BRIGHT RED pipe comes from the lung balloons down through the roof into the top-RIGHT room.
- A BRIGHT RED pipe leaves the exit door of the thick-walled bottom-RIGHT room and runs to the right, to a big friendly man standing at the right edge of the picture, and enters his chest.
- Two GREY-BLUE pipes come back from the man to the top-LEFT room: one starts at the man's head and arches over the top of the house, coming down into the top-left room from above; the other starts at the man's legs, runs on the ground under the house between the stilts, and comes up into the top-left room through its outer left wall.

No other people. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels.
