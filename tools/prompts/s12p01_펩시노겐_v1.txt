One square illustration in a hand-inked storybook style: thin dark brown-black ink outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, a warm cream paper background, muted colours. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. / THE SETTING: A large hollow chamber shaped like a STOMACH - a curved bean-shaped room seen in cutaway, with a pale blue-grey stone wall enclosing it and a sandy floor inside. A narrow doorway leads out at the upper right. / THE TWO KINDS OF WORKMEN - THIS IS THE BACKBONE OF THE PICTURE: Inside the chamber stand about eight bare-chested workmen, and every one of them has a normal human upper body but NO legs - his lower half is a solid object he stands on. There are exactly two kinds and they must never be mixed: (A) COLUMN MEN, whose lower half is a fluted pale grey GREEK STONE COLUMN with a carved capital; (B) BRICK MEN, whose lower half is a squat pillar of RED BRICKS with visible mortar lines. Roughly four of each, scattered around the chamber so both kinds are easy to count and compare. / WHAT EACH KIND POURS - AND THE ONE NEW THING: Every COLUMN MAN holds a dark green-glass COLA BOTTLE - the classic soft-drink bottle with a narrow neck, a fluted curved waist and a crimped metal crown cap - and tips it so a thick, pale, lumpy BATTER glugs out and puddles on the floor. The bottles are large and unmistakable, held high and clear of the men's bodies so their shape reads at once. Every column man has one. / Every BRICK MAN instead tips a plain round-bellied clay JUG from which a thin CLEAR liquid pours in a bright transparent stream, splashing where it lands. No brick man holds a cola bottle; no column man holds a jug. / THE ENVELOPES: Only the BRICK MEN carry a cream paper ENVELOPE tucked under one arm. One brick man is handing his envelope to a running errand boy who is carrying it out through the far doorway at the upper right. No column man has an envelope. / OUTSIDE THE WALL: On the ground outside the stone wall, on both sides, stand two low basins, each fed by a brass TAP set in the outer wall; frothy white bubbles fill the basins. / THE CALF: At the lower left, inside the chamber, a small brown-and-white CALF stands beside a wooden table on which a wide bowl holds thick CURDLED MILK with a wooden paddle stuck in it. / WHAT MUST NOT BE IN THE PICTURE: no letters, numbers or writing on the bottles, walls or envelopes; no labels of any kind; no man with ordinary legs; no column man holding a jug and no brick man holding a bottle. / WHAT MUST BE TRUE WHEN YOU ARE DONE: every workman's lower half is either a Greek stone column or a stack of red bricks, never anything else. Every column man pours thick pale batter from a green glass cola bottle with a crimped cap. Every brick man pours clear liquid from a plain clay jug and only brick men carry envelopes, one of which is leaving by the far door. Two frothing basins fed by taps sit outside the wall, a calf and a bowl of curdled milk at the lower left. One square picture, warm cream paper, thin hand-drawn linework, no lettering of any kind.
