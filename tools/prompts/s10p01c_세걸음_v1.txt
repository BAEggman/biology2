Draw a single illustration in this style - warm cream paper, thin hand-inked storybook outlines, soft flat watercolour washes, muted greys, tans and dusty blue-greens, on bare cream ground, no text of any kind. Wide image, about three times as wide as it is tall. Keep it simple and uncluttered.

WHAT TO DRAW - THREE square framed pictures in a row, side by side, separated by plain thin vertical gutters, like three panels of a comic strip. Every one of the three shows THE SAME scene: a low grey-green stone BLOCK with exactly TWO square open BAYS cut into its front, a LEFT bay and a RIGHT bay, and a pair of parallel RAILS running horizontally beneath the block. Only what is happening in the bays changes from panel to panel.

FIRST PANEL (left): the RIGHT bay is EMPTY - nothing in it at all. A single workman is dropping STRAIGHT DOWN from above into the LEFT bay, his feet just reaching its floor, with small motion lines above him showing he came from directly overhead and not from the side. He holds ONE single round BEAD in his hand. Nothing is in the right bay.

SECOND PANEL (middle): now there is a workman in EACH bay. The LEFT workman holds out a long STRING OF BEADS - about eight beads on a cord - and is handing that whole string RIGHTWARD to the workman in the RIGHT bay, who takes it and joins it onto the ONE bead he is already holding. The string plainly passes from left hand to right hand, and the LEFT workman's other hand is EMPTY and open, palm up.

THIRD PANEL (right): the LEFT bay holds a workman with empty hands. In the RIGHT bay there is NO person at all - instead a plain flat rectangular wooden BOARD has slid into that bay and sits there, blank, with no face, arms or legs. A long string of beads is falling out of the block and down onto the ground below, loose and no longer held by anyone.

Nothing else is in the picture. No other people, no animals, no tools, no machines, no background.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN: any number of framed panels other than three; the block having any number of bays other than two in any panel; the rails missing under the block in any panel; in the first panel, a workman appearing in the right bay, or the left workman walking in from the side instead of dropping straight down; in the second panel, the string of beads being handed from right to left instead of left to right; in the second panel, the left workman still holding beads in his other hand - that hand must be plainly empty; in the third panel, the board being drawn as a person, a robot or a face, or the board being placed in the left bay instead of the right; in the third panel, the string of beads still being held by someone instead of falling free; any writing, letters, numbers, labels or arrows appearing anywhere.
